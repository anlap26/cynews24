Permanent residence in Cyprus through a €300,000 investment

Regulation 6(2) is an immigration permit tied to an investment of at least €300,000 and to a secure income. It is not citizenship and it is not tax residence.

Редакционная группа CyNews 24Опубликовано: 27 ноября 2026, 09:00 (EET)Обновлено: 27 ноября 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Permanent residence through an investment is an immigration permit under Regulation 6(2) of the Aliens and Immigration Regulations. The Migration Department publishes the criteria. The version it points to is the fourth revision, from May 2023, and the department has said that new applications are examined on the criteria in force when the file is submitted. This page follows that public text. It is not a decision, and an agent’s summary from an earlier year is not a substitute for the investor criteria.

What the permit is

The permit is a right to reside, for the applicant and for the family members the criteria allow, tied to an investment that continues to qualify. It is not citizenship. It is not a passport. It is not tax residence: the 183-day rule and the 60-day rule are a Tax Department question, explained in taxes for individuals. It is also not, by itself, permission to take a job. What a holder may do as a director or a shareholder is set out in the same criteria. Read that section before you accept a contract.

The money for the investment has to be shown as transferred to Cyprus from abroad, from an account of the applicant or of a spouse who is included in the application. The department expects the price to move from the buyer’s account to the seller’s account at a financial institution in Cyprus.

The investment

The applicant invests at least €300,000 in one of the categories the criteria list. They are not interchangeable.

CategoryWhat you buyNew or resaleIncome
A. House or apartmentFrom a development company, at least €300,000 plus VATFirst sale onlyAt least €50,000 a year, from abroad
B. Other propertyOffices, shops, hotels or a mix, totalling €300,000Resale is allowedThe criteria allow income from activity in Cyprus as well
C. Company shares€300,000 into a Cyprus company with a real presence and at least five employeesA new company or an increase of capitalIncome may come from the activity, within the criteria
D. Fund units€300,000 in units of a Cyprus collective investment scheme whose investments are held in the RepublicAs the criteria describeAs the criteria describe

A house bought from a private owner, rather than from a developer on a first sale, does not meet category A. VAT sits on top of the €300,000 in that category. It is not included in the €300,000. A unit marketed at €300,000 including VAT is a different and smaller investment. The purchase sequence, the title and the fees are in buying property.

If the holder sells the investment and does not replace it at once with another that meets the same rules, the permit can be cancelled. That sentence is in the criteria. It is the part of the file that continues after the approval letter.

What a first sale means

A unit bought from a private owner is not category A, even at the same price. The VAT is extra, not part of the €300,000.

Income

On top of the investment, the applicant shows a secure personal annual income of at least €50,000. Add €15,000 for a spouse and €10,000 for each dependent minor child of the applicant or the spouse. The spouse’s income may be counted. Salaries, pensions, dividends, interest and rents are the kinds of income the criteria name.

For category A, that income has to come from abroad. The criteria say it is proved by a tax return from the country where the applicant declares tax residence, or, where the department’s clarifications allow it, by a certificate from an independent certified accountant. For categories B, C and D, part or all of the income may come from activity inside Cyprus. Do not copy the category A rule onto a shop, or the other way round.

Adult children are not the same as minor children. Dependent students aged 18 to 25, and adult children who apply in their own right, follow the extra rules in the criteria, including, in some cases, a further investment. Read those paragraphs rather than assuming a child of 22 is covered by the parent’s €10,000 add-on.

Minor children and adult children

A child of 22 is not covered by the €10,000 add-on. The criteria split students aged 18 to 25 from minor children.

The other conditions

The applicant and the spouse provide a clean criminal-record certificate from the country of origin and from the country of residence if that is different. They must not be a threat to public order or public security. Health insurance is required where GeSY cover is not available. The department’s list of accompanying documents is the list that matters on the day you file. As of 2026, download it from the criteria page rather than from a translated copy on a forum.

The property itself still has to be a lawful purchase. A non-EU buyer needs the acquisition permission the Ministry of Interior describes, on top of this immigration permit. One approval does not silently include the other. The limits are summarised in buying as a foreigner.

After the approval

Keep the investment. Keep the evidence the department asks you to keep. Secondary guides disagree about how often income has to be shown again: some still say every year, and a 2026 reading of the department’s clarifications says the annual income resubmission was dropped while the investment itself must still be maintained, with criminal-record evidence on a longer cycle. This page will not pick a winner between those readings. The safe step is to follow the timetable on the current criteria and on any letter the department sent with the permit.

A sale, a gift, or a transfer of the unit into a company can break the category even when the family still lives in the house. Ask before you restructure. Replacing the investment is allowed when the replacement meets the rules. Doing it quietly is not.

The permit does not set the tax position. A holder who spends the year in Cyprus will often become tax resident under the 183-day rule. A holder who does not live in Cyprus is not tax resident merely because the permit exists. Non-dom status, which is about Special Defence Contribution and not about this permit, is explained in the tax guide. Sketch a number on MyCyTax only after you know which test you meet.

Choosing the property

Category A is a home. The city changes the life more than the permit does. Limassol, Paphos, Larnaca and Nicosia are compared in where to live. A first-sale apartment with a separate title already issued is a simpler file than a unit that will be carved out of a mother title in three years. Ask for the title copy, the planning permit and the VAT treatment before you treat the brochure price as the investment amount.

Useful services

  • A lawyer who acts for the buyer, for the title, the contract and the lodging at the Land Registry. Immigration counsel is a related brief. They are not always the same person.
  • The developer’s lawyer acts for the developer. That is a reason to have your own.
  • Health insurance that satisfies the criteria until GeSY, if GeSY becomes available, is discussed in the health insurance guide.
  • A Cyprus account is often the account the purchase funds land in. What banks ask for is in opening a bank account.

Вопросы читателей

Is €300,000 the whole cost of a flat?

For a house or apartment the criteria ask for a first sale of at least €300,000 plus VAT. VAT is on top. A resale home does not meet that category.

What income do I have to show?

At least €50,000 a year, plus €15,000 for a spouse and €10,000 for each dependent minor child. For a house or apartment the income has to come from abroad.

Can I work?

The permit is not a work permit. What a holder may do as a director or shareholder is in the same criteria. Read that section before you accept a contract.

What if I sell?

If you dispose of the investment and do not replace it with another that meets the rules, the permit can be cancelled. That is in the criteria.

Does the permit make me tax resident?

No. Days in Cyprus, and the 60-day conditions if you rely on them, decide tax residence. A permit holder who does not live in Cyprus is not tax resident merely because the permit exists.
