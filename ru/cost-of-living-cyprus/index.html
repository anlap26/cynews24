Cost of living in Cyprus: a monthly budget for rent, food and bills

Rent decides the city. Electricity decides whether the flat was a bargain. There is no official monthly cost of living, so this page shows ranges and says what moves them.

Редакционная группа CyNews 24Опубликовано: 27 ноября 2026, 09:00 (EET)Обновлено: 27 ноября 2026, 09:00 (EET)

The cost of living in Cyprus is a rent bill, a food bill, an electricity bill and, for most households outside a walkable centre, a car. There is no official “monthly cost of Cyprus”. The ranges below are the sort of numbers a household actually meets in 2026. They move. Treat them as a way to build your own budget, then check the tariff or the listing.

Rent, which dominates

Rent is the line that decides the city. A one-bedroom asked at roughly €1,200 to €2,200 in Limassol, €800 to €1,400 in Paphos, €700 to €1,200 in Larnaca and €700 to €1,300 in Nicosia is the middle of the long-term market, not a seafront penthouse and not a village room. Two-bedrooms sit higher. The detail, the deposit and the lease are in renting an apartment. The character of each city is in where to live.

A deposit of one or two months, plus the first month, is cash you need at the start and should not bury inside the monthly budget. It comes back, if the lease is honoured. Common-building charges of €50 to €200 a month are common in a block with a lift and a pool, and rare in an old walk-up. Ask.

Deposits are not a monthly cost

One or two months’ deposit is cash at the start. It should come back. Do not hide it inside the monthly number.

A month, sketched

The table is one person and a couple, renting, not owning. Owning replaces rent with a mortgage or no housing bill, and adds building insurance, a management fee and maintenance. It does not add up to “free”.

Add private school if you have children. Fees at English-language schools often run from several thousand euros a year to well above €10,000, and the popular ones are not priced like the public system. See schools. Add health insurance for any month you are not inside GeSY, and a GeSY contribution once you are. See GeSY.

Owners and tenants

The table is for a household that pays rent. An owner swaps the rent line for maintenance, insurance and any mortgage.

Groceries

Supermarkets on the island stock the same European brands people know, at prices that sit with southern Europe rather than with the cheapest markets on the continent. A weekly shop for one person that is mostly ordinary food, not imported specialities, is what the €250 to €400 band assumes. A household that buys all fruit out of season, all meat as a named import, and a delivered meal three nights a week will leave the band quickly.

Municipal markets are cheaper for produce in season and useless as a reason to ignore the supermarket. The winter tomato and the August tomato are not the same price. As of 2026, the statistical service publishes a consumer-price index. Use it to see the direction of food prices. Do not use a single blogger’s receipt as the index.

Electricity

The Electricity Authority of Cyprus bills on a tariff that changes. Air-conditioning is the reason a July bill and a November bill do not belong in the same sentence. A modest, well-shaded flat used by one person can sit near the bottom of the range. A glass apartment with the units running all afternoon sits at the top or above it. The tariff, and any fuel-adjustment clause the authority is applying, is on eac.com.cy. As of 2026, check it before you sign a lease on a flat with no shutters and a western aspect.

Heating in Nicosia and in the hills is the winter version of the same surprise. A coastal flat may barely use heat. An inland flat in January will. Price the city you chose, not an average of the island.

Getting around

A car is the default outside central Nicosia and the most walkable bits of the coastal towns. Fuel, insurance and a roadworthiness test are the owning costs, set out in driving in Cyprus. Hiring for a month while you decide is often dearer than people expect and is explained in the car hire guide. Buses are cheap and, for a weekly shop or a school run in Paphos, often impractical. Budget the car if you are not sure. Removing it later is easier than discovering in August that you need one.

Owning instead of renting

A purchase replaces rent and adds transfer fees or VAT, which are one-off, and maintenance, which is not. The one-off charges are in buying property and can be sketched on MyCyTax. A mortgage payment at a local rate can be lower or higher than the rent of the same flat. Compare the two on the actual interest rate a bank offers you, not on a forum’s example. The bank’s requirements are in opening a bank account. A mortgage is a separate credit decision. An account is not an approval.

Council tax in the British sense does not appear as a single annual bill. You will see municipal charges, a sewerage charge and the building’s own fund. Ask the seller or the landlord for the last year of those bills. They are part of the cost and they are knowable.

What not to add

Do not add a “visa cost” as if every route had one fee. MEU1 is a small fee. A visitor file has the fees on the form plus a bank guarantee, which is your money tied up, not a fee. Do not add income tax as a cost of living. Tax depends on residence and on taxable income, which the tax guide separates from the supermarket. A person who is not tax resident does not have a Cyprus income-tax bill merely because they rent a flat. A person who is tax resident might.

The honest monthly number is the table, filled with your rent and your bills, plus the annual items divided by twelve: insurance, school, a flight home if that is part of your life, and the road tax. If that number is uncomfortable, the city is wrong or the flat is wrong. The residence route will not fix it. The routes are in moving to Cyprus.

Вопросы читателей

What should one person budget besides rent?

Groceries of roughly €250 to €400, electricity of €80 to €250 depending on the season, and €40 to €90 for water, rubbish and internet. A car is extra. These are 2026 ranges, not a tariff.

Why is electricity so variable?

Air-conditioning. A shaded flat and a glass flat are different bills in August. The tariff is on the Electricity Authority’s site.

Is Limassol always the dearest?

For rent, yes, at the middle of the market. A village house can look cheaper until you add the car and the distance to a school or a hospital.

Does owning remove the monthly cost?

It removes rent and adds maintenance, insurance, a management fee and any mortgage. The one-off taxes on the purchase are separate.

Should I add income tax to the budget?

Only if you are tax resident and have taxable income. The tax guide is a different page. A rent payment does not itself create a tax bill.
