Opening a bank account in Cyprus, and what an EU payment account is not

A licensed Cyprus bank and an EU payment app do different jobs. Landlords, migration files and bank guarantees usually mean the first.

Συντακτική ομάδα CyNews 24Δημοσιεύτηκε: 27 Νοεμβρίου 2026, 09:00 (EET)Ενημερώθηκε: 27 Νοεμβρίου 2026, 09:00 (EET)

A bank account in Cyprus is a local account at a credit institution the Central Bank licenses, opened under the anti-money-laundering rules every EU bank applies, sometimes slowly. An account at a payment institution or an electronic-money institution elsewhere in the EU is a different product. People use both. They are not substitutes, and a landlord, the Migration Department or an employer who asks for a Cyprus account is asking for the first of them.

What “a Cyprus bank” means

The retail banks residents actually use include Bank of Cyprus and the other credit institutions on the Central Bank’s list. The list, not a blog’s shortlist, is the authority for who is licensed. It is published by the Central Bank of Cyprus. Names change when banks merge. If a guide names a bank that has since been absorbed, the licence sits with the successor, and the account-opening pack may still carry the old brand for a while. Check the list as of 2026.

A branch will usually want you in person for a new relationship, with the originals of the documents, not scans. Some banks open for non-residents and some do not, or they do only above a balance, or only through a named unit. There is no right to an account at a particular bank. If one declines, another may accept. A decline is not an appealable immigration decision. It is a commercial one, and the bank does not have to explain it in the detail you would like.

The register, not a shortlist

The Central Bank publishes who is licensed. A merger can change the name on the door. The licence is the fact that matters.

What they ask for

The pack is know-your-customer. Expect to be asked for the following, and expect a second round.

  • A passport, and a residence document if you have one. A stamp alone is a weaker file than a permit. Some banks wait until the permit is issued. Others open a limited account earlier. Ask that bank.
  • Proof of address. A lease, a title, or a recent utility bill. A letter from a hotel is what it is. The lease standards are in renting an apartment.
  • A tax number, or a tax-residence certificate from another country, so the bank can meet its reporting duties. Tax residence itself is explained in taxes for individuals. The bank’s form does not make you tax resident.
  • Source of funds and source of wealth, in proportion to what you will move. Salary slips, a pension letter, sale contracts, company accounts. A large transfer with a one-line story is the transfer that sits in compliance for weeks.
  • A reason for the account: salary, household expenses, a property purchase, a company. The reason should match the money.

The bank will also ask you to sign its tariff. Fees for a current account, for a card and for an incoming international transfer are not uniform. Read the tariff. This page does not invent a monthly fee, because the banks change them.

A second round is normal

Compliance often asks again after the first appointment. Translate and certify before you go, not in the corridor.

How long it takes

A straightforward salaried file with a permit, a lease and a clean payslip can be done in a couple of weeks. A file with several companies, a third-country source of wealth, or documents that need translation can take a month or two, and sometimes longer. Build that into the plan. A property completion that depends on the account being open on a Friday will slip if compliance has a question on the Wednesday. The purchase steps are in buying property. The investment route, which expects the price to land in a Cyprus institution, is in permanent residence.

Translate documents before the appointment if they are not in Greek or English. Certify what the bank asks to have certified. A pretty scan of an uncertified document is the usual reason for a second appointment.

EU payment and electronic-money accounts

Revolut, Wise and similar services are available to many people who are resident in the EU, and Cyprus is in the EU. They are useful. A euro account number, a card, and a low-cost transfer are real features. They are regulated as payment institutions or electronic-money institutions, generally in another member state, not as a Cyprus bank. That distinction matters in four places.

  • A checklist that says “a bank account in the Republic” is not satisfied by an IBAN that happens to start with a different country code, even if you live in Larnaca.
  • A bank guarantee for a visitor permit is issued by a bank in Cyprus. A payment app does not issue it. The visitor file is in the pink slip guide.
  • Deposit protection, where it applies, follows the scheme of the country that licenses the institution, up to that scheme’s limit. Read which scheme, and which limit, on that institution’s own page. Do not assume the Cyprus deposit-guarantee figure covers a balance held with a firm licensed in Lithuania or Belgium.
  • A large incoming transfer from a third country can be questioned by the payment firm just as a bank would question it. The firm can freeze the transfer while it asks. Keep the same evidence of source.

Use the payment account for ordinary spending if it suits you. Open the local account when a person or an office in Cyprus has asked for one, or when you want a relationship that can later include a mortgage or a guarantee. Doing both is normal.

Companies and the personal account

A personal account and a company account are different applications. The company needs its own documents: incorporation, directors, a description of the business, and often a business plan the bank’s unit actually reads. The site’s note on opening a company is the corporate side. Do not run household expenses and client receipts through one account because it was the one you could open. It is the pattern compliance teams are told to look for.

If the account is refused

Ask what is missing, fix that, and try again or try another bank. Do not shop the same incomplete pack around the island in the same week. A refusal is sometimes “not with these documents” and sometimes “not this profile”. A lawyer or an accountant who already works with a particular bank can say which of the two it is. They cannot force the opening.

Keep records of what you submitted. If a later permit file asks where your money sits, you will want the statements. And keep the foreign account that already receives your pension or salary until the new one has been used, successfully, for a month. Closing the old account on the day the new card arrives is how people strand a transfer.

Useful services

  • The Central Bank’s register, to confirm the institution is licensed in Cyprus.
  • A translator for the pack, before the appointment rather than in the corridor.
  • The MyCyTax pages if the reason for the account is a purchase and you want the transfer fees and VAT sketched first.
  • The health insurance guide is unrelated to the account, except that some banks ask for a local address and a local phone before they will treat you as resident. Insurance does not open the account. A lease does more.

Property search and a document check

Banks ask for the lease or the title. If you are about to sign either, and you want it read before it becomes the proof of address in a bank pack, use the property consultation. Say whether you are renting or buying, and name the city. The form asks for a name and an email. It does not open the account and it is not a mortgage offer. Ερωτήσεις αναγνωστών

Which banks can I use?

Credit institutions licensed by the Central Bank of Cyprus. The list is on the Central Bank’s site. Mergers change the brand. The licence is what matters.

What will they ask for?

Passport, a residence document if you have one, proof of address, a tax identifier, and evidence of where the money comes from. The thicker the wealth, the longer the questions.

Is Revolut or Wise a Cyprus bank account?

No. They are payment or electronic-money services, usually licensed in another EU country. They are useful. A checklist that says a bank in the Republic is asking for something else.

How long does it take?

A simple salaried file can be a couple of weeks. A complicated source of funds can be a month or more. Do not schedule a property completion against an account that is not open.

What if I am refused?

Ask what is missing and try again with a complete pack, or try another bank. There is no right to an account at a named institution.
