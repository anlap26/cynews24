The Cyprus digital nomad visa: income, steps, length and tax

A permit for remote work for a foreign employer or foreign clients: at least €3,500 a month net, a cap of 500, and a first card for one year.

Συντακτική ομάδα CyNews 24Δημοσιεύτηκε: 27 Νοεμβρίου 2026, 09:00 (EET)Ενημερώθηκε: 27 Νοεμβρίου 2026, 09:00 (EET)

The Cyprus digital nomad visa is a temporary residence permit for a national of a country outside the EU and the EEA who works remotely, through telecommunications, for an employer registered abroad or for clients located abroad. The Migration Department is accepting applications again. It says the maximum number of permits remains 500. The page to trust is digital nomads and family members. The form and the document list are on the forms page. The form is MVIS8, the same shell used for other temporary permits, with a nomad document list behind it.

Who qualifies

Three conditions sit on the department’s page.

  • The person can do the work remotely, through telecommunications.
  • The person is employed by a company registered abroad and can work without being in a particular place, or is self-employed and provides services to clients located abroad.
  • The person can prove stable monthly net income of at least €3,500, after contributions and taxes.

The accompanying-documents list, in the version dated 19 March 2025 and still the list linked from the department, raises that income by 20 percent for a spouse or partner and by 15 percent for each child. A couple with one child is not a €3,500 file. Do the arithmetic from the list, not from a headline that quotes only the single-person figure. As of 2026, check the list, because the department revises the PDF without changing the slogan.

The work must not be for an employer in Cyprus and must not be services to a client established in Cyprus. A foreign contract that is, in substance, a Cyprus client is the point officers look at. The scheme is not a way to take a local job.

Family members, meaning a spouse or civil partner and minor children, can reside for the same period. They do not get the right to work or to carry on an economic activity. A spouse who wants to work needs a permit that allows it. This one does not.

The income is a household figure

A spouse adds 20 percent and each child adds 15 percent to the €3,500. Quote the single-person number only if you are applying alone.

The steps

Apply within three months of arrival, at the department’s central offices in Nicosia, unless you already hold a different lawful status and the page allows a change of status from inside the country. Check whether an appointment is required. The page says to look that up rather than to assume a walk-in.

Bring the passport, valid for at least three months after the application, and the page that shows the last entry. An employee brings a contract that lasts at least as long as the permit requested, a letter from the employer that the work can be done remotely and does not concern a Cyprus employer, the foreign company’s registration, and contact details. A self-employed person brings contracts of at least that length and a statement that the clients are not established in Cyprus. Everyone brings evidence of the net income: payslips or the equivalent, and bank statements, typically the last six months, showing that the income is stable and comes from outside the Republic. Where the papers do not show the net figure, the list asks for an affirmation.

Documents need the translation and the certification the list describes. Biometrics are taken at submission. The department says examination takes about five to seven weeks. That is an estimate on the page, not a promise to you.

Where you file

The department’s central offices in Nicosia, within three months of arrival, unless a change of status from inside the country is allowed. Check whether you need an appointment.

How long it lasts

A first permit is issued for one year. A renewal can be issued for up to two further years. The department describes the benefit as residence for a year with the possibility of renewal for a further two years, so three years in all if the renewals are granted. A renewal is filed in Nicosia at least one month before the permit expires, with the fee the page states and with updated evidence if the employer or the contracts have changed.

There is no published path from this permit to permanent residence. Three years is the horizon the page describes. A person who wants to stay longer needs a different basis: employment, a family status, or the investment route in permanent residence, each with its own rules. Staying on after the permit and hoping the next scheme will cover the gap is not a strategy this page recommends.

Tax, which is not the permit

The department’s own page says that a person who resides in the Republic for one or more periods totalling more than 183 days in the same tax year is a Cyprus tax resident, provided they are not tax resident anywhere else. That is the 183-day rule, stated in a migration document. The Tax Department’s full test, including the day-counting rules and the 60-day rule, is on tax residency and domicile and is explained in taxes for individuals.

A nomad who is in Cyprus most of the year should expect to be tax resident. A nomad who uses the permit for a few months and spends the rest of the year elsewhere may not be. The permit does not decide it. Neither does the employer’s country. If the 60-day rule is the one you hope to use, remember that it requires a business, employment or office in Cyprus, which sits awkwardly next to a permit that forbids work for Cyprus. Do not assume the 60-day rule is available. Read it.

Tax residence brings filing. From the 2026 tax year a resident aged 25 to 70 files a return even with no Cyprus-source income. Employment income can qualify for an exemption for first employment in Cyprus. The percentage, the salary threshold and the number of years have been amended more than once. This page does not copy a threshold. Read the current Tax Department note, and run your own numbers on MyCyTax. Non-dom status, which is about defence contribution on dividends and interest, is a separate question and is not granted by this permit.

Social insurance and GeSY follow if the person becomes insured in Cyprus. A nomad employed abroad may remain insured in the other country for a period, under that country’s rules and any agreement. Do not cancel foreign cover on the day the permit arrives. GeSY enrolment for residents who qualify is in the GeSY guide.

Accommodation and the practical year

The file needs an address, as the other residence files do. A lease of a real flat is the ordinary proof. Short stays stacked for a year read as what they are. Rent ranges and the lease terms are in renting an apartment. The city changes the rent more than the permit does. See where to live.

A bank account helps, and some applicants already have the foreign account that receives the salary. A Cyprus account is a separate project, described in opening a bank account. Open the foreign statements now. Six months of gaps is a hard thing to repair in the week before the appointment.

Useful services

  • The employer’s letter, on the company’s paper, matching the list. A generic “to whom it may concern” that does not say the work is remote and not for Cyprus is the letter that comes back.
  • Health insurance for the months before any GeSY enrolment, compared in the health insurance guide.
  • A workspace and a reliable connection. That is a private purchase. It is not part of the permit.
  • Tax calculators on MyCyTax, after you know whether the 183-day test is met.

Property search and a document check

If you need a lease that will stand up as proof of address, or you are weighing a purchase against a year on this permit, use the property consultation. Say that you want to rent or to buy, and name the city. A one-year permit is a weak reason to buy unless you have another plan for the years after it. The form asks for a name and an email. It does not prepare the nomad file. You can also write to [cynews24@gmail.com](mailto:cynews24@gmail.com).

Ερωτήσεις αναγνωστών

How much do I need to earn?

The department says at least €3,500 a month net, after tax and contributions. The document list adds 20 percent for a spouse or partner and 15 percent for each child.

Can my spouse work?

No. Family members can reside for the same period and cannot take employment or carry on an economic activity on this permit.

How long does it last?

One year at first. A renewal can be for up to two further years. The page describes three years in all if the renewals are granted. It is not permanent residence.

Will I be tax resident?

If your days in Cyprus exceed 183 in the tax year and you are not tax resident elsewhere, the department’s own page says yes. The permit does not decide it by itself.

Can I serve a client in Cyprus?

No. The work has to be for an employer or clients established abroad. A contract that is really a Cyprus client is the point the file looks at.
