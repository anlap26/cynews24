The Cyprus visitor permit: the pink slip, MVIS8 and proof of funds

The pink slip is a temporary permit for a third-country national who will not work. The form is MVIS8, and the income figure is the one on the current checklist.

Συντακτική ομάδα CyNews 24Δημοσιεύτηκε: 27 Νοεμβρίου 2026, 09:00 (EET)Ενημερώθηκε: 27 Νοεμβρίου 2026, 09:00 (EET)

The visitor permit is the residence document people call a pink slip. It is for a third-country national who will stay in Cyprus without working. The form is MVIS8. The Migration Department publishes the form and the supporting-documents list on its visitor forms page, and a shorter description on visitors and family members. This guide follows those pages. A copy of the list saved last year is not the list.

Who it suits

It suits a person who can support a household from income earned outside Cyprus, and who does not need to work for a Cyprus employer or for Cyprus clients. Retirees with a pension, people living on savings or on rent from property elsewhere, and accompanying family members who will not take a job are the ordinary cases. It does not suit someone who has a job offer. That file is an employment permit. It does not suit someone whose work is remote for a foreign company: that is the digital nomad visa, with a higher income test and a different list. It does not suit an EU citizen, who uses MEU1. The routes side by side are in moving to Cyprus.

The permit is temporary. It is renewed. It is not permanent residence and it is not a path that quietly becomes one. The investment route is a different application, described in permanent residence from €300,000.

It is not the other permits

An EU citizen uses MEU1. A remote worker looks at the nomad scheme. A person with a job offer needs an employment permit.

When to file

File while the stay is still lawful. The department expects the application before the visa or the visa-free period runs out. Cyprus is not in Schengen, so the 90-in-180 calculator people use for the Schengen area is the wrong clock. Count the days on the Cyprus stamp or the Cyprus visa. An application made after the stay has already expired is a worse file.

The application can be lodged at the Migration Department in Nicosia or, for districts other than the capital, at the Aliens and Immigration unit of the police, as the form itself explains. Biometrics are taken in person. A representative cannot do that part for you.

The papers

The form is one MVIS8 for each family member. Download it from the department. Documents in a foreign language need a certified translation into Greek or English, and the certificates the list names need the legalisation the list names. The usual groups are these.

  • Passport, valid well beyond the application date, and the page that shows the entry.
  • Accommodation: a lease, a title, or a host declaration if someone is putting you up. The lease should be a real tenancy of a home you can live in, not a week in a serviced room. What landlords ask for is in renting an apartment.
  • Income from abroad. The checklist quoted from February 2026 asks for at least €2,000 a month or €24,000 a year for one person, increased by 20 percent for a spouse and 15 percent for each child, shown as transfers into an account in the applicant’s name. A first filing has also accepted a cash import of at least €10,000, declared at customs, in place of a long transfer history. Confirm the current numbers on the list you download. As of 2026, the file is the authority, not this paragraph.
  • A bank guarantee from a bank in Cyprus, one for each family member, against the cost of a possible repatriation. The amount depends on the country of origin. The list sets the bands and the validity. A guarantee that is about to expire will not cover a renewal.
  • Health insurance that meets the list. GeSY is generally not available on a visitor stamp alone. Medical insurance for the gap is discussed in the health insurance guide.
  • On a first application, a criminal-record certificate and the medical tests the list names, which have included hepatitis, HIV, syphilis and a chest X-ray. Renewals do not always repeat every test. Read the renewal column, not the first-application column.

Fees are set in the legislation and printed on the form. Do not budget from a forum. The receipt is the figure.

What the permit does not allow

You cannot work. You cannot invoice a Cyprus client and call it a hobby. A business activity is a different permit, and doing it on a visitor permit is a reason the renewal fails. Volunteering and remote work are not loopholes this page will invent. If the plan is remote work for a foreign employer, use the nomad scheme if you qualify, or do not come on this permit.

A visitor permit also does not make you tax resident. Tax residence is the 183-day test or the full 60-day test, explained in taxes for individuals. Many visitors do cross 183 days and then have a filing question even though they still hold a pink slip. The slip is not a reason to ignore the return. From the 2026 tax year a tax resident aged 25 to 70 files even with no income. Sketch the position on MyCyTax and then read the Tax Department’s page.

The guarantee and the medical tests

The guarantee amount depends on the country of origin, and a first filing asks for medical tests that a renewal may not repeat. Use the column that matches the application you are making.

Renewal

A renewal is the same permit, not a new life. File before the card expires. The department’s list asks for the lease or the title again, for evidence that the foreign income is still arriving, and for insurance that still covers the next period. The bank guarantee can often be left in place if it still has enough time on it. The balance the list wants to see in the account is not the same figure as the first-application deposit. Read the renewal rows.

A gap between expiry and the new card is uncomfortable. You may be lawful because an in-time application is pending, and you may still be unable to board a flight or to satisfy a bank. Ask the unit what evidence of a pending renewal they issue, and do not plan a trip that depends on the new card being in your hand on a particular Friday.

A clean file

Use your own account, your own name on the transfers, and an address you can take an officer to. A friend who “adds you to the lease” the week before the appointment is visible in the paperwork. So is a deposit that arrived from a third party. The declaration of income that accompanies the statements should match the statements.

If the household includes a spouse or a child, each person has a form, a guarantee and a place in the income calculation. Leaving a child off the file and then enrolling the child in school is how the two systems collide. Schools and the permit are separate, and both will ask where the child lives. See schools.

Useful services

  • A translator the Press and Information Office would recognise, for any document that is not in Greek or English.
  • A Cyprus bank account, because the guarantee and the income trail usually run through one. What the banks ask is in opening a bank account.
  • Medical insurance for the year of the permit, compared in the health insurance guide.
  • A car only if the district needs one. Owning it is the driving guide. Hiring while you wait is the car hire guide.

Property search and a document check

The permit needs a real address. If you are choosing a lease, or you want a contract checked before you pay a deposit that you will then show the department, use the property consultation. Say that you are renting for a visitor file, and name the city. The form asks for a name and an email. It does not prepare the MVIS8 and it does not predict the decision. Ερωτήσεις αναγνωστών

Who should use this permit?

Someone who can live on foreign income and will not work in Cyprus. A job offer, remote work for a foreign firm, and an EU passport are all different routes.

What income does the checklist ask for?

The list quoted from February 2026 asks for at least €2,000 a month or €24,000 a year for one person, plus 20 percent for a spouse and 15 percent for each child. Download the current list before you rely on that.

Can I work?

No. Economic activity needs a different permit. A renewal fails when the officer can see that you worked anyway.

Does the permit make me tax resident?

No. Crossing 183 days can. The permit and the tax test are separate, and a tax resident aged 25 to 70 files a return from the 2026 tax year even with no income.

When do I file?

While the stay is still lawful. Cyprus is not in Schengen. Count the Cyprus stamp, not a Schengen calculator.
