A new Cyprus resident’s checklist: tax code, yellow slip, GESY and the return

It is not one card. A 2026 handbook sets out the tax code, the yellow slip and the pink slip, GESY, the income-tax return, social insurance, a bank account and the driving-licence exchange, with the fee or deadline each department prints.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 17 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 17 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

A move to Cyprus does not end with one card. The tax code, the yellow or pink slip, GESY, the income-tax return, social insurance, a bank account and a driving licence are separate files. A 2026 handbook links each one to the authority’s own page. This is that map, on the rules as published for 2026, not an assessment or an immigration decision.

The tax identification code is the number on the Tax Department’s register. Joining is only through Tax For All: a portal account, then a taxpayer registration, with the documents on the Department’s list. The code is not a social insurance number, not an alien-registration number, and not a decision on tax residence. https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/tic/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides

Yellow slip is form MEU1, the registration certificate for an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen. The Migration Department says the application is made within four months of entry, and the fee is €20. Pink slip is form MVIS8, a visitor’s temporary permit for a third-country national: €70 to issue or renew it, and another €70 for a first registration in the Aliens’ Register if the person is not already registered. A yellow slip is not a third-country permit. A pink slip does not allow work. Neither document is tax residence. https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/residence-registration/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides

GESY needs two steps, the Health Insurance Organisation says: the beneficiary registry, and a personal doctor’s list. One without the other does not open the list. A non-Cypriot needs a migration record and a valid residence permit first. https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/gesy/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides

The Tax Department puts the 2025 income-tax return at 31 October 2026. From the 2026 tax year the return is filed only in Tax For All, so the 2025 return is not held for that system. From 2026 a Cyprus tax resident aged 25 to 70 files even with no income, and filing needs the tax code. https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/income-tax-return/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides

A social insurance number is for a person who is gainfully occupied, employed or self-employed. Arrival does not create one, and a visitor who may not work stays outside the compulsory scheme. Business in Cyprus states the split as 8.8% from the employee and 8.8% from the employer. https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/social-insurance/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides

A bank account is not a loan. The Central Bank of Cyprus leaflet on a payment account with basic features gives a consumer who legally resides in the EU a right of access to one. It does not fix a document list for every bank. https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/bank-account/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides

A foreign driving licence is exchanged after the Road Transport Department’s test: at least 185 days of normal residence. The application is in person. https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/driving-licence/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides

A car and the utilities are separate files. Customs clears a vehicle not yet on the Cyprus register, the Road Transport Department registers it before it is licensed, and a car already registered changes keeper on another form. Electricity is an agreement with the Electricity Authority of Cyprus. Water is the local board or community. A residence card replaces neither a title nor a lease. Car: https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/register-a-car/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides Electricity and water: https://mycytax.com/guides/resident-handbook/utilities/?utm_source=cynews24&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=guides
