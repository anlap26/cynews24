Affiliate disclosure

What an affiliate link is

Some practical guides on CyNews 24 link to companies whose products a reader who lives or travels abroad may actually use: international health insurance, a travel eSIM, a VPN, encrypted email, or a password manager. If you follow one of those links and buy, the company may pay CyNews 24 a commission. You do not pay an extra fee to us. The price, if there is one, is the price on the company’s own site.

Where the links live

Every partner address is stored in one configuration file, source/affiliates.json. The site publishes that file as /assets/js/affiliates.js, and every partner button reads the address from there. Until a real address is added, the value is a placeholder such as #AFFILIATE_GENKI. A placeholder does not go to a shop. The partners named in the guides are Genki, Airalo, NordVPN, Surfshark, Proton (VPN, Pass and Mail) and 1Password. Some practical pages also link to Amazon.de.

Amazon

Those Amazon links are searches on Amazon.de, not a page for one product, and they do not name a price. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. The home address carries the tag cynews24-21, and a search uses the same tag. CyNews 24 does not invent a product code. What Amazon shows, and what it costs, is on Amazon at the time you look.

Travel homepages

Travel guides may link to Booking, GetYourGuide, DiscoverCars, Airalo and SafetyWing. Those five addresses live in one file, source/travel_affiliates.json, under the names BOOKING_URL, GETYOURGUIDE_URL, DISCOVERCARS_URL, AIRALO_URL and SAFETYWING_URL. Until a real affiliate address is pasted there, each value is the company’s own homepage. A homepage is not a tracked offer and it does not name a price. The button is still marked sponsored.

How to recognise one

A partner button opens in a new tab and is marked rel="nofollow sponsored". The guide says, near the offers, that the links are affiliate links, and it links to this page. News stories do not carry these buttons. A short box appears only on a few practical pages about moving, expat life, banking or technology, and it links back to the full guide.

What the guide does not do

The text does not invent a premium, a coverage limit, a server count or a statistic. Where a figure is not in a source we cite, the guide says to read the current terms, because they change. A partner link is not a recommendation that a product is right for you, and it is not insurance, legal, tax or security advice. CyNews 24 is not affiliated with the companies, except that we may earn a commission if you buy through a link.

Corrections

If a guide is wrong, email cynews24@gmail.com with the page address and the correction. The editorial policy explains how corrections are handled.
