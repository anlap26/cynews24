GeSY in Cyprus: registering, a personal doctor and private cover

GeSY is the national health system. You enrol, you choose a personal doctor, and you pay the co-payment the organisation has set. A separate policy still covers the gaps.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 27 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 27 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

GeSY is the General Healthcare System, the national scheme run by the Health Insurance Organisation. Once you are inside it, you choose a personal doctor, you are referred on when you need to be, and you pay the co-payment the organisation has set for that visit. Before you are inside it, you pay privately or you rely on a policy. This page is how enrolment works for a resident, and why a separate policy still has a job. The organisation’s site is gesy.org.cy. As of 2026, the contribution rates, the co-payments and the enrolment screens are the ones on that site. A guide goes stale. The site does not.

Who can register

Beneficiaries are defined in the law: citizens, and other residents who meet the insurance conditions, including many people who hold a residence permit and who pay contributions or who are insured as dependants. A tourist stamp is not the status the system was built for. A visitor permit, a nomad permit or an employment permit each interact with GeSY differently, and the migration record has to be able to identify you. The practical advice the site has already published for foreigners is GeSY for foreigners. Read it next to this page. If the two ever disagree, the Health Insurance Organisation’s own rule wins.

EU citizens who carry an S1, or who remain insured in another member state for a period, should not cancel that cover and assume GeSY has replaced it on the day they collect a yellow slip. Coordination between systems is a Social Insurance and HIO question. Ask it with the documents in your hand.

How to enrol and choose a doctor

Enrolment is online, through the beneficiary portal, once you have the identity the system accepts. That is often the residence permit number together with the alien registration or civil identification the migration step produced. The screen asks you to choose a personal doctor, a general practitioner, from the list of doctors who still have room on their list. The choice is yours, within that availability. A doctor in the neighbourhood who is full is not a doctor you can select by insisting.

The personal doctor is the front door. You book there for ordinary care. The doctor refers you to a specialist inside the network when the condition needs it. Specialists can be seen without a referral in the cases the organisation lists, and not otherwise. Accident and emergency is for emergencies. Using it as a same-day GP clinic is how the co-payment and the waiting room both get worse.

The lead photograph is Nicosia State Hospital, the largest public hospital. It is not a picture of your GP’s office, and a hospital in Nicosia is not a reason to skip a personal doctor in the town where you live. Paphos, Larnaca and Limassol have their own hospitals. The city comparison is in where to live.

You book ordinary care there. A specialist visit normally follows a referral. Accident and emergency is not a same-day clinic.

Contributions

GeSY is funded by contributions on income, not by a premium you shop around. The rates that have been in force for several years, and that the organisation still publishes, are 2.65 percent for employees, 2.90 percent for employers, 4 percent for the self-employed, 2.65 percent for pensioners, and 2.65 percent on other income such as rent, interest and dividends, with the state paying its own share. There is an annual ceiling on the income that is charged. Commentaries put that ceiling at €180,000 across the relevant categories. Check the current ceiling on gesy.org.cy before you budget a percentage of a large dividend. As of 2026, the page is the rate. This paragraph is the reminder that a rate exists.

The contribution is not optional for a person who is a beneficiary with income of that kind. Non-dom status, which removes Special Defence Contribution on dividends and interest, does not remove the GeSY contribution. The tax position is in taxes for individuals. A sketch of income tax, which is a different bill, is on MyCyTax.

The ceiling is published, not guessed

Commentaries often cite €180,000. The organisation’s page is the ceiling to use in a budget.

Appointments and co-payments

Book the personal doctor through the route that doctor uses: the GeSY system, the clinic’s phone, or both. A referral to a specialist produces an appointment, not necessarily a fast one. Popular specialities have waits measured in weeks. The co-payment for a GP visit, a specialist visit, a lab test or a medicine is a small regulated amount, set by the organisation and printed before you confirm. It is not a bill for the full cost of the care. If you go outside the network, you pay the private price, and GeSY does not reimburse it just because the doctor was good.

Take the referral, the identity document and a list of medicines to the first specialist visit. Repeat prescriptions of a medicine you used in another country are not automatic. The personal doctor has to be willing to continue them, and the medicine has to be one the system covers, or you pay privately for that item.

Why a separate policy still helps

GeSY covers a great deal and it does not cover everything a household from abroad is used to having on demand. The reasons people keep a second policy are practical.

  • The months before enrolment is open, including a long visitor-permit wait. A travel policy and a longer-stay policy are different products. The comparison is in international health insurance.
  • A specialist wait you are not willing to sit through, for a condition you already know about. Private clinics in Limassol and Nicosia can see you quickly, at a private price.
  • Dental and optical care beyond the narrow slice GeSY includes. Most of that spending is private.
  • Care while you are outside Cyprus. GeSY is a Cyprus system. It is not travel insurance for a trip to another country, and the European Health Insurance Card rules are a separate coordination question.
  • A room, a doctor or a procedure the public list will not schedule on your timetable. Paying for it is a choice. Describe it as a choice, not as a failure of the system to exist.

A second policy should be read for exclusions, for pre-existing conditions, and for whether it pays the hospital or pays you back. The guide linked above refuses to invent a premium. So does this one. Get the premium from the insurer, for your age and your history.

Children, dependants and the papers

A child is enrolled as a dependant of an insured parent, with the child’s own documents. A school will ask for evidence that the child can be treated. Sort the enrolment before the first week of term if you can. Schools are a different system, described in schools and kindergartens. The residence permit of the parent is often what the health system and the school both end up asking to see. The permit routes are in moving to Cyprus.

Questions readers ask

How do I join?

Through the beneficiary portal once your identity and residence record match what the system expects. You then choose a personal doctor who still has room on the list.

What does it cost?

Contributions on income, at rates the Health Insurance Organisation publishes, not a premium you shop. The rates long in force include 2.65 percent for employees. Check the current rate and the income ceiling on gesy.org.cy.

Can I see a specialist directly?

Only in the cases the organisation allows. Otherwise the personal doctor refers you. A private visit outside the network is a private bill.

Does non-dom status cancel GeSY contributions?

No. Non-dom status is about defence contribution. GeSY contributions on dividends, interest and rent can still apply.

Why keep another policy?

For the months before you can enrol, for dental and optical care beyond the scheme, for treatment abroad, and for a wait you do not want to sit through.
