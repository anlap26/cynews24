Flights to Cyprus: Larnaca, Paphos, and what a search does not decide

The Republic's international airports are Larnaca and Paphos, both run by Hermes Airports. A fare is whatever the search shows today. Ercan is not a Republic airport. ETIAS is not on sale.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

A flight to Cyprus lands, for the Republic, at one of two airports. Larnaca and Paphos are the international airports, and Hermes Airports is the company that operates them. Larnaca's code is LCA. Paphos's code is PFO. Which one is closer depends on the town you are going to, not on a slogan about the island as a whole. Limassol and Nicosia are commonly reached from Larnaca. Paphos town and the western resorts are the reason Paphos airport exists. That is geography, not a ranking of airlines.

What this page will not invent

It will not name a carrier as the one that flies your route. Schedules change, and a guide that freezes them is wrong by the next season. It will not print a fare. A search on the day you travel is the fare. Aviasales is the flight search this site is set up to link. The button opens that search. CyNews 24 does not add a markup in the sentence, and it does not know the price.

Search flights to Cyprus

It will not tell you that a particular month is cheap. Weather by month describes climate, which is not the same as a fare. School holidays in the country you leave from move the price more reliably than a paragraph about sunshine.

The airport that is not an entry to the Republic

Ercan, also called Tymbou, sits in the north. It is not an airport of the Republic of Cyprus. A ticket there is not described on this page as an arrival at Larnaca or Paphos. The practical consequence people run into is not a fare. It is the question of which border recorded the entry, which is set out in Cyprus and Schengen in 2026. This guide does not advise anyone to arrive in the north in order to reach the Republic.

Papers before the search

Whether your passport can board is a question for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic, not for a search box. A Schengen visa and permission to enter Cyprus are not the same document while Cyprus is outside Schengen. ETIAS, the future travel authorisation, is not collecting applications. The Commission's position, and the place where official pages disagree about Cyprus, is in EES and ETIAS in 2026. Pay the airline and, if a visa is required, the authority that issues it. Do not pay a private page for an authorisation the Commission has not opened.

Questions readers ask

Which airports count as entry to the Republic?

Larnaca and Paphos are the international airports of the Republic, operated by Hermes Airports. Their codes are LCA and PFO. A ticket to a different airfield is not, on this page, described as an arrival in the Republic.

Why do people mention Ercan?

Ercan, also called Tymbou, is in the north and is not an airport of the Republic. The Schengen guide explains the recording problem that creates for a future border system. It is not a suggestion to use it.

How much is a flight?

This page does not print a fare. Fares move by day and by airport of origin. Aviasales is a search, and the price you see there is the price at that moment, not a promise from CyNews 24.

Do I need ETIAS to board?

Not while the European Commission says ETIAS is not collecting applications. Read the EES and ETIAS guide before you pay a website that claims otherwise. A visa, if your passport needs one for the Republic, is a separate question for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

How do I leave the airport?

A pre-booked car, a taxi and a bus are different products. The site's transfer guide and the car-hire guide cover the practical side. Kiwitaxi and Welcome Pickups are affiliate buttons, not a statement that one firm is on strike or not.
