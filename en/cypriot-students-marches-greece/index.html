Cypriot students in Greece marched on Turkish missions on the anniversary of the "TRNC"

On Sunday, the 43rd anniversary of the unilateral declaration, there were marches in Athens, Komotini and Thessaloniki. In Thessaloniki police kept the columns back from the consulate.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 16 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 16 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

On Sunday 15 November, Cypriot students in Greece marched on Turkish diplomatic missions. The day was the 43rd anniversary of the unilateral declaration of the "TRNC" on 15 November 1983. Reporter and Sigmalive note that only Turkey recognises that entity.

In Athens, Reporter writes, students marched towards the Turkish embassy, as in previous years, with flags and slogans. In Komotini, members of the Cypriot Students' Union of Komotini left a protest resolution outside the Turkish consulate. The march set off from the old rectorate. The participants, Reporter and Sigmalive report, citing Ta Nea, were protesting the continuing occupation of the north of the island.

In Thessaloniki, TyposThes reports three separate marches towards the Turkish consulate. Columns with flags and banners moved along Agiou Dimitriou street. At the junction with M. Kyriakou street, police put up a barrier so that marchers would not get close to the consulate. When the Pancyprian Unified Organisation of Students (PEOF) of Thessaloniki reached that point, participants set fire to a structure spelling out "ΔΕΝ ΞΕΧΝΩ" ("I do not forget") and to a flag of the "TRNC". A three-day symbolic hunger strike by Cypriot students opposite the consulate had begun on 13 November; PEOF holds it every year.

A separate report by Karfitsa describes another column in Thessaloniki: a gathering at the corner of Agias Sofias and Tsimiski and a march towards the consulate with Greek flags and banners, and with slogans against the occupation and against a bizonal federal settlement. Police blocked the approach. Marchers tried to hand over a resolution and were not allowed to. In the text, the paper writes, partition of the island was rejected and union with Greece was supported; it also said the occupation continues and refugees remain away from their family homes. The march ended with the national anthem, and the participants then left peacefully.
