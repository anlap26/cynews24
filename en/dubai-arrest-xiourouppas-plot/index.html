Wanted suspect in alleged Limassol murder plot arrested at Dubai airport, reports say

The case concerns an alleged €800,000 plot to kill Giorgos Xiourouppas; Cypriot authorities are still waiting for official confirmation from the UAE, the same reports say.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 16 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 16 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

A Greek Cypriot man wanted in connection with an alleged €800,000 plot to murder Giorgos Xiourouppas in Limassol has been arrested at Dubai airport while trying to leave the United Arab Emirates, according to information obtained by Philenews. The reports do not name the man who was detained. The paper says he is the second of two people wanted in the case.

According to information received by Philenews, the man had said he intended to return to Cyprus after media reports linked him to the investigation, but UAE authorities detained him as he tried to leave Dubai. A source familiar with the inquiry confirmed to the paper that Cypriot authorities had been told of the reported arrest but were still waiting for official confirmation from their UAE counterparts. Efforts are under way, the same source said, to obtain permission for the suspect to leave Dubai for Cyprus; whether that will be possible remains unclear, because the UAE authorities are also examining the case.

No European or international arrest warrant has yet been issued for either of the two wanted suspects, Philenews notes. The other wanted person is of Arab origin, the paper says, and further developments concerning him are possible. Four suspects, aged 48, 36, 32 and 73, are already in custody in Cyprus in connection with the alleged plot.

Police are investigating an alleged €800,000 contract killing targeting Xiourouppas. According to evidence presented in court, the alleged agreement included an advance payment of €400,000. Investigators are also looking into the alleged shipment of Semtex explosives in connection with the suspected plot. The inquiry, Philenews writes, has also uncovered allegations involving the two wanted individuals reported to be in Dubai.

Other Cypriot outlets, including Sigmalive and Reporter, likewise report that one of the wanted suspects was detained at Dubai airport and that the Cypriot side is still awaiting official confirmation from the UAE. This account reflects reports published by the morning of Monday, 16 November.
