ONE Limassol: what Pafilia publishes about the tower on 28th October Avenue

An independent ONE Limassol review. This is not a Pafilia listing. The developer’s pages say 37 storeys, 83 homes and a start of €1,700,000 plus VAT. In 2021 Pafilia wrote about first handovers. Its 2026 guide still offers what remains.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 21 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 21 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

This is an independent reading of Pafilia’s public texts on ONE, the tower on 28th October Avenue in Limassol. CyNews 24 is not selling these homes and has no partnership with Pafilia. Figures below are the developer’s. They can change. VAT is stated only where a page itself writes “plus VAT”.

A Pafilia article from 2023 calls ONE a 37-storey residential seafront tower on 28th October Avenue and says 83 apartments, no more than three on a floor. The same text gives a start of €1,700,000 plus VAT. Pafilia’s investment guide, in a file whose address carries a September 2026 date, repeats 37 storeys, 83 homes, the same start plus VAT, and a line that a limited number of premium units remain. Two of the developer’s own documents agree on those figures. That is firmer than a project whose site and sales desk print different counts.

“The tallest residential seafront tower in Europe” is a phrase in the 2023 article. The 2026 guide says “the tallest residential tower of its kind”. Those are Pafilia’s descriptions, not a check this article made against other towers. The German project page names Atkins, WKK, HBA and Buro Happold on the team, and says WKK took the curve of a yacht as a reference. The English guide, in the passage we read, does not list the architects by name.

The mix the German page describes: two- and three-bedroom apartments, three- and four-bedroom maisonettes, and a three-storey penthouse. The 2026 guide says an apartment runs from one side of the tower to the other, sea on one side and the city and Troodos on the other, and it repeats the cap of three homes on a floor. Amenities in the guide: a 24-hour reception, a concierge, a secure car park, bicycle parking, an outdoor pool with a deck, a gym, a spa with a treatment room, a sauna and a steam room, a meeting room, a business centre, a children’s pool area and a terrace. Those English texts do not give an energy class, and none is invented here.

The timetable is not one picture. Pafilia’s 2021 note “ONE progress” says construction was almost complete, testing and commissioning were under way, and the first clients were taking delivery of sky villas. The German page’s wording is handover since 2021. The September 2026 guide still offers a start of €1,700,000 plus VAT and a limited remainder. On the company’s own account, some homes had already been handed over and some were still for sale in 2026. Which unit is which is not a choice the page makes for a buyer.

What the published record supports: one developer, one address on 28th October Avenue, matching figures of 37 storeys and 83 homes, a price with the words “plus VAT” in two Pafilia documents, and an amenity list. What it does not settle: “tallest in Europe” is not checked here, the pages do not fix one date for the whole tower, and €1,700,000 is a start, not the price of a typical home.

ONE suits a buyer who wants an apartment on central Limassol’s seafront with a hotel-style service list and a budget from the published €1.7 million plus VAT, and who will check whether a given floor is free. It is a poor fit for someone looking for a house outside the city, or for an entry price well below that line.

Before a contract, the specification of one unit matters: whether it has been handed over, what finish remains, and whether a parking space is included. PwC’s Cyprus summary puts ordinary VAT at 19%. It describes the 5% rate as the first 130 m², up to a value of €350,000, if total buildable area is not over 190 m², the transaction is not over €475,000, and the home stays the main residence for ten years. This is not tax advice. The Department of Lands and Surveys says a transfer that is subject to VAT pays no transfer fee, and a transfer that is not subject to VAT gets a 50% reduction of that fee. The Tax Department sets capital gains tax at 20% on a profit from disposing of immovable property in Cyprus, with listed exemptions. A start of €1,700,000 plus VAT is above the €475,000 transaction ceiling in that summary, so the 5% rate described there does not attach to such a price. Ask which of the 83 homes have already been delivered and which remainder is still on the list. The form under this article goes to CyNews 24, not to Pafilia.

Questions readers ask

How many storeys and homes does ONE have?

Pafilia’s 2023 article and its 2026 guide both say 37 storeys and 83 homes, with no more than three on a floor.

What is the price?

Both Pafilia texts say from €1,700,000 plus VAT. The 2026 guide adds that a limited number of premium units remain. That is not a price for every apartment.

Is the tower finished?

In 2021 Pafilia wrote that the first clients were taking delivery. The September 2026 guide still offers a remainder. Those texts do not pick one completion date for the whole tower. Check the specific unit with the developer.

Is this a Pafilia listing?

No. The form under the article goes to CyNews 24, not to the developer.
