Cyprus non-dom tax in 2026: what the status actually exempts

Non-dom is a Special Defence Contribution status, not a discount on salary. Dividends and interest are the usual point. The 17-year rule can end it. The 60-day test changed on 1 January 2026.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

"Non-dom" is a label people attach to a move. In Cyprus law it is narrower. The Tax Department's page on residency and domicile says Special Defence Contribution is charged to an individual who is a Cyprus tax resident and who also has a domicile in Cyprus. A person who is not domiciled, or who is not tax resident, is not subject to SDC on interest or on dividends. That is the exemption. It is not a waiver of income tax on a salary, and it is not a residence permit.

Domicile is not a passport

The Department says a domicile of origin is normally acquired at birth and, as a general rule, follows the father's domicile at that time, with exceptions it describes for a child born outside marriage or after the father's death. A domicile of choice is a matter of settling somewhere with the intention of staying permanently or indefinitely. The page says the domicile of origin continues until a domicile of choice is acquired. None of that is decided by which passport is in the drawer. This guide does not decide anyone's domicile.

There is a second door, which does not depend on origin. The Department says that anyone who has been a Cyprus tax resident for income-tax purposes for at least 17 of the last 20 years before the tax year is deemed to have a Cyprus domicile. That deemed domicile stays until the person completes 20 years of not being Cyprus tax resident. A newcomer with no earlier Cyprus tax years does not meet 17 out of 20 in year one. Years already spent as a tax resident count. The arithmetic belongs in a file, not in a headline that says "17 tax-free years".

What the exemption touches

On the Department's page, the non-domiciled person is outside SDC on interest and dividends. Employment income is taxed under the income-tax rules if the person is tax resident, or under the source rules if they are not. PwC's Tax Facts and Figures 2026, a summary of the amended law rather than the statute, states the 2026 bands: nil on the first €22,000 of chargeable income, 20 percent to €32,000, 25 percent to €42,000, 30 percent to €72,000 and 35 percent above €72,000. Confirm the band on the assessment, not from this paragraph.

PwC also describes a 50 percent exemption for remuneration from first employment in Cyprus that starts after 26 July 2022, for people who were not Cyprus residents for at least three consecutive tax years immediately before and who were employed abroad by a non-resident employer. The booklet sets conditions and a period. It is not applied here to a named reader. The older 20 percent and 50 percent exemptions, PwC says, do not apply to jobs that started after 26 July 2022, though someone already inside an older exemption may have a remaining period. Read the current text before you put a number in a contract.

Rental income needs a caution. PwC's 2026 booklet and a Legal 500 note of 3 November 2026 say SDC on rent ended on 1 January 2026. The Tax Department's page still discusses rental income and attaches a note that a short quotation can cut off. Until you have read that note on the live page, do not treat rent as automatically free of SDC because a secondary summary said so. Income tax on rent is a separate question from SDC.

The 60-day rule after 1 January 2026

Tax residence is the other half. The Department's page sets out the 183-day rule and the 60-day rule, including how a day of arrival and a day of departure are counted. The 60-day rule on that page requires at least 60 days in Cyprus, no more than 183 days in any other single country, a business, employment or office in Cyprus, and a permanent home in Cyprus that the person owns or rents. If the business or the job ends during the year, the Department says the person is not treated as tax resident for that year under the 60-day rule.

PwC and the Legal 500 note say Law 244(I)/2025, published on 31 December 2025, removed a further condition from 1 January 2026: the person no longer has to be free of tax residence in every other state. The condition about 183 days in any one other state remains, on their account and on the Department's list. A treaty can still break a tie. The day counts and the filing point are in Cyprus tax residency in 2026.

A residence card does not create this status. The immigration routes are in EU residence in Cyprus. A company is a different taxpayer: the corporate rate is discussed in opening a company, and it is not the non-dom exemption.

Questions readers ask

Does non-dom mean I pay no income tax?

No. Income tax and Special Defence Contribution are different charges. Non-dom status, as the Tax Department describes it, takes a non-domiciled person out of SDC on interest and dividends. Employment income is an income-tax question.

How long does it last?

The Tax Department says a person who has been Cyprus tax resident for at least 17 of the last 20 years is deemed domiciled, whatever the domicile of origin. Someone with no earlier Cyprus tax residence usually meets that only after 17 such years. Earlier years in Cyprus can bring it forward.

What changed on 1 January 2026?

Law 244(I)/2025, as described by PwC and by a Legal 500 note, removed the condition that a person using the 60-day rule must not be tax resident anywhere else. The Tax Department's page still requires 60 days in Cyprus, no more than 183 days in any other single country, a Cyprus business, job or office, and a permanent home that is owned or rented.

Is rent free of SDC in 2026?

PwC's 2026 tax facts and the Legal 500 note say SDC on rental income ended on 1 January 2026. The Tax Department page discusses rental income with a note. Read the current Department page before you rely on a zero.

Is this personal advice?

No. Domicile of origin is a legal idea under the wills and succession rules. A passport does not decide it. A filing position needs the Tax Department or an adviser who will sign it.
