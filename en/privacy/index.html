Privacy policy

Who we are

This policy covers the CyNews 24 website. The publisher is Anna. The site is based in Cyprus. Email: cynews24@gmail.com.

Advertising contact form

The Advertise & Contact page has a form that sends your message through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to cynews24@gmail.com. The fields are your name, company, email, phone if you provide one, the formats you select, an optional budget range, your message and your consent. We use those details only to reply to that enquiry. FormSubmit processes the submission in order to deliver the email. We do not sell the details and we do not add them to a marketing list.

Analytics

The site does not use Google Analytics. It loads Google AdSense (ca-pub-1547877294743851) so Google can show ads. AdSense may receive technical data such as the page address and a cookie identifier.

Cookies

CyNews 24 does not set its own cookies. The pages load Google AdSense, which may set advertising cookies. The language chooser on the front page reads the browser language and may redirect once; it does not store a cookie. If you follow a link to another site, that site has its own policy.

Email

If you email cynews24@gmail.com, we use the message to answer you or to correct a story. We keep it for as long as that purpose requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
