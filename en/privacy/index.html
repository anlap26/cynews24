Privacy policy

Who we are

This policy covers the CyNews 24 website. The publisher is Anna. The site is based in Cyprus. Email: cynews24@gmail.com.

Advertising contact form

The Advertise & Contact page has a form that sends your message through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to cynews24@gmail.com. The fields are your name, company, email, phone if you provide one, the formats you select, an optional budget range, your message and your consent. We use those details only to reply to that enquiry. FormSubmit processes the submission in order to deliver the email. We do not sell the details and we do not add them to a marketing list.

Analytics

The site does not use Google Analytics. It loads Google AdSense (ca-pub-1547877294743851) so Google can show ads. Advertising and analytics storage stay denied until you accept cookies in the banner. If you accept, AdSense may receive technical data such as the page address and a cookie identifier. If you reject, that storage stays denied. Cookie settings in the footer opens the choice again.

Cookies

CyNews 24 does not set its own cookies. The accept or reject choice is stored in this browser's local storage under the name cynews24-consent, which is not a cookie. Google Consent Mode starts denied, before the AdSense script and before any analytics tag. AdSense may set advertising cookies only after you accept. The language chooser on the front page reads the browser language and may redirect once; it does not store a cookie. The cookie policy has the full account. If you follow a link to another site, that site has its own policy.

Email

If you email cynews24@gmail.com, we use the message to answer you or to correct a story. We keep it for as long as that purpose requires.

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
