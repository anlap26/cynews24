Hundreds Dress in White as Limassol Bids Farewell to 17-Year-Old Schoolgirl

Mourners dressed in white gathered in Limassol on November 24 for the funeral of 17-year-old Kyriaki Anastasiou as authorities await lab test results.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 24 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 24 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Family, classmates, and public officials gathered in Limassol on Tuesday, November 24, to pay their final respects to 17-year-old Kyriaki Anastasiou, who died suddenly during a school trip to Kakopetria over the weekend. ​The funeral service took place at the Church of Agios Nikolaos in Kato Polemidia. Following the family's wishes, mourners—including Education Minister Athena Michaelidou and senior police officials—wore white attire to the ceremony. ​The exact cause of the teenager's death remains undetermined. An initial autopsy proved inconclusive, and authorities are awaiting the results of specialized laboratory and toxicological tests. ​The tragedy has sparked widespread public debate across Cyprus regarding safety protocols, supervision during overnight school excursions, and emergency response procedures.
