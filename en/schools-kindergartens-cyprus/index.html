Schools and kindergartens in Cyprus: public, private and international

Public school is free and taught in Greek. An English-language school is a private contract, with a fee and, at the known names, a waiting list.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 27 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 27 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

School in Cyprus is free if you use the public system and you accept that the language of the classroom is Greek. It is a private market, with fees and waiting lists, if you want English or another language as the medium of instruction. Kindergartens follow the same split, a year or two earlier. This page is the map. It is not an offer of a place at any named school, and a fee printed here would be out of date before the next intake. Ask the school. As of 2026, the Ministry of Education’s pages are on moec.gov.cy.

A child who will stay for many years and who is young enough to learn Greek quickly can thrive in the public system, and many do. A child who arrives at fourteen with no Greek, into a programme that assumes the language, is in a different position. Be honest about which child you have. The romantic version of “they will pick it up” is sometimes true at six and often painful at fifteen.

The photograph is the Pancyprian Gymnasium in Nicosia, a historic public school. It is not an advertisement of a place, and a photograph of one building is not the admission rule.

Language is the real difference

A six-year-old can learn Greek in a public classroom. A fourteen-year-old with no Greek is a different decision. Be honest about the child you have.

Public schools

Public pre-primary, primary and secondary schools take children who live in the Republic on the terms the ministry sets. The school is usually the one for the address. That is a reason to know the address before you promise a child a particular building. Moving district can mean moving school. The curriculum leads towards the Cypriot leaving certificate. Greek is not a subject at the edge of the day. It is the day.

Support for pupils who arrive without Greek exists in some schools and is not a full international curriculum hiding inside the public one. Ask the head, for that school, what a newcomer in your child’s year actually receives. Get the answer in time to decide, not in October.

The address picks the public school

Public places follow where you live. Choosing the flat and assuming the school is how families end up driving across a city.

Private and international schools

Limassol has the widest choice of English-medium schools, which is one reason families pay Limassol rents. Nicosia has a long-established group of English-medium and private schools around the capital. Larnaca and Paphos have options, fewer of them, and the good ones fill. The cities are compared as places to live in where to live. Do not sign a lease that assumes a school place. Sign the lease after the school has offered one, or accept that you may be driving across a district or changing plan.

Admissions, in outline, look like this.

  • An application a long way ahead of the year you need. For the popular Limassol schools, a year ahead is ordinary and still not a guarantee.
  • Previous school reports, and sometimes an assessment in English and in mathematics.
  • A place offered for a year group that may not be the year the child’s age suggests, if the school thinks the child needs it.
  • A deposit to hold the place, which is the school’s money under the school’s terms. Read whether it comes back if you change your mind.
  • Fees billed by term or by year, plus bus, lunch, uniform and exam entries. The headline fee is not the whole cost.

A school that follows the English curriculum, the International Baccalaureate or an American programme is preparing a different leaving certificate from the public school next door. Universities in the UK and elsewhere understand those certificates. Say which university system you are aiming at before you choose the programme. Changing at sixteen is possible and is not free of cost or of lost ground.

Kindergartens

Public pre-primary education covers the ages the ministry defines, in Greek, and is the normal path into public primary. Private nurseries and kindergartens take younger children and children whose parents want English, and they are a private contract with a fee and a notice period. Visit at the time of day you would actually drop the child off. A lobby at 10:00 and a door at 07:45 are different places.

Ask about ratios, about whether the staff stay, and about what happens in August, when many Cypriot businesses slow down and a working parent’s August does not. A nursery that closes for four weeks is a childcare gap, not a detail.

Papers the school will ask for

A passport, previous reports, a vaccination record, and evidence of the address. Some schools ask for the parent’s residence document because they have been left with a child whose family’s stay was never regularised. The document is not the school’s to grant. The routes are in moving to Cyprus. Health cover for the child, whether through GeSY once you are enrolled or through a policy before that, is in the GeSY guide. A school nurse is not a health system.

If a grandparent or a member of staff will collect the child, put that in writing with the school. The expectation in many buildings is stricter than newcomers assume.

Money

Public school removes the fee and does not remove the cost of living in the district that contains the school you want. Private school adds a fee that can exceed the rent. Put the fee in the monthly budget by dividing the year, and add the bus if you will not do the drive. The budget around it is in the cost of living. A household that can pay Limassol rent and two school fees is making a different choice from a household that can pay one of those. Do the sum before the viewing, not after the child has seen the playground.

Useful services

  • The ministry site, for the public-school year and the compulsory ages.
  • The admissions office of each school you are seriously considering, by email, so you have the answer in writing.
  • A Greek-language option, even alongside an English school, if the child may stay. A Saturday class is cheaper than a lost decade of the local language.
  • The property consultation if the school and the flat have to be chosen together.

Property search and a document check

Families move for a school and then discover the flat is forty minutes away in term-time traffic. Questions readers ask

Is public school free?

Yes. The language of the classroom is Greek, and the school is usually the one for your address. It is not a hidden international programme.

How much are private schools?

Often from a few thousand euros a year to well above €10,000, plus bus, lunch and exams. Ask the school for the current fee. This page does not print one.

Which city has the most choice?

Limassol, then Nicosia. Larnaca and Paphos have fewer English-medium places, and the known schools fill. Get an offer before you sign a lease that assumes a desk.

When should I apply?

A long way ahead of the year you need. For the popular schools, a year ahead is ordinary and still not a guarantee.

What papers does a school ask for?

Passport, reports, a vaccination record and proof of address. Some ask for the parent’s residence document. The school cannot issue that document.
