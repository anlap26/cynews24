Social media only from 15, even with parental consent: Cyprus puts the bill out to consultation

The government has published a draft law that would bar children under 15 from creating or keeping social media accounts. Platforms would have to check users' ages. Views are accepted until 11 December.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

The government has put out to public consultation a bill that would set a minimum age of 15 for social media accounts in Cyprus. Cyprus Mail, Politis and the English service of Philenews reported the start of the consultation on Wednesday, 25 November. The draft is titled the Protection of Minors in the Use of Online Social Networking Services Law of 2026. It was prepared by the Interior Ministry with the Office of the Law Commissioner, according to its page on the e-consultation.gov.cy portal.

Under the draft, children under 15 could not create or keep a social media account, even if their parents or guardians agree. The platforms themselves would be responsible for enforcing the age limit. They would have to use age verification systems that respect privacy and protect personal data. There are transitional arrangements for existing accounts, and users would have to be informed, Politis reported.

The law covers only social media, not everything children do online. Online encyclopaedias and educational and scientific platforms are excluded. Supervision would go to the Radio-Television and Digital Services Authority, as the national Digital Services Coordinator. It could impose administrative measures and fines on platforms that break the rules.

“Social media offers significant opportunities for communication, learning, expression and creativity. At the same time, exposure to inappropriate content, cyberbullying, constant social comparison and the effects of excessive use on children’s daily lives create new challenges,” President Nikos Christodoulides wrote on X, as quoted by Cyprus Mail.

The bill is part of the first of three pillars of the action plan for protecting children on social media, which the Council of Ministers approved on 16 September 2026. The other two are technological tools and education and awareness. The government said, as reported by Politis, that the aim is not to keep children away from technology but to protect them from the risks of social media. It also said Cyprus is among the European countries that have drafted a specific bill on an age limit.

Comments can be sent through e-consultation.gov.cy until Friday, 11 December 2026. The Citizen's Voice platform is separately collecting the public's views.
