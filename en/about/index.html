About CyNews 24

Who we are

CyNews 24 is an independent trilingual Cyprus news aggregator and digest. It is published in Russian, English and Greek, and every story is available in all three languages. The site is based in Cyprus. The publisher is Anna. CyNews 24 is not affiliated with the outlets it cites.

Mission

Our mission is to give readers a clear, sourced briefing of what is happening in Cyprus, in the language they read. We select distinct, newsworthy reports and rewrite them so the same facts can be read in Russian, English and Greek.

Coverage

We cover Cyprus politics, the economy, real estate and planning, society, tourism, sport, and world news with a Cyprus connection. A separate Events section lists confirmed upcoming events. We do not publish every brief: a story has to be distinct and newsworthy.

CyNews 24 Editorial Team

Stories carry the byline CyNews 24 Editorial Team. We do not use individual reporter bylines, and this page does not name members of staff. For editorial questions, corrections or tips, email cynews24@gmail.com.

How stories are produced

We follow reporting from Cypriot and international outlets and write each article as an original rewritten summary. Facts come only from the cited sources. We do not invent numbers, quotes or events. Every article ends with a Sources box that links to the originals.

Photographs are real pictures under open licences that allow commercial use and adaptation — CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, from Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash or Pexels. They are never taken from news sites. Each picture is credited with its author, source and licence and links to the original file. Where the licence is CC BY-SA, the cropped and watermarked version stays under that same licence. A picture shows the place or subject of a story; it is not a photograph of a person named in the article unless the credit says so.
