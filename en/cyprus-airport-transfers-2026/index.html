How to leave Larnaca and Paphos airports in 2026: bus, taxi or a pre-booked car

The two international airports, the Kapnos and Limassol Airport Express shuttles, the fixed airport-taxi fares, and what still runs if the flight lands at night.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Cyprus has two international airports, Larnaca (LCA) and Paphos (PFO). Hermes Airports, which runs both, names the bus operators on its public-transport page and says taxis wait around the clock. What follows is what those operators publish. A fare that is not in a source is not invented here.

A pre-booked car with a name board

A private car and driver is booked before the flight. The driver meets you in the arrivals hall rather than in the taxi queue. It is the practical choice when the flight is late, the party is large, the luggage is more than a bus allowance, or the address is not a shuttle stop. The company states the price on the day you book. This page does not.

The licensed airport taxi

Hermes says taxis wait on the right as you leave the arrivals hall at level 0, and that the service runs 24 hours. Fixed fares to and from the airports were set by ministerial notice K.D.P. 79/2021. Hermes publishes that notice. The fares apply from 1 March 2021, include VAT and the carriage of luggage, and also apply on public holidays. The day rate runs from 06:00 to 20:30. The night rate runs from 20:30 to 06:00.

For a taxi of up to four passengers, the notice sets these fares from Larnaca airport: Nicosia €50 by day and €55 at night; Limassol €55 and €65; Ayia Napa €50 and €60; Protaras €55 and €65. The Paphos coastal areas the notice puts on one line (Kato Paphos up to the Tombs of the Kings, Chloraka up to Kissonerga, and Coral Bay up to west of Pegeia) are €105 by day and €125 at night. From Paphos airport, up to four passengers: Kato Paphos up to the Tombs of the Kings €20 and €25; Chloraka up to Kissonerga €25 and €30; Coral Bay up to west of Pegeia €35 and €40; Limassol €60 and €70; Nicosia €115 and €135; Larnaca €105 and €125. A taxi for five or six passengers has a higher column in the same notice. The full table is the PDF on the Hermes site. If a later notice has replaced this one, the desk follows that notice, not a memory of this page.

Kapnos Airport Shuttle

Kapnos says on its own site that it has run a scheduled service from both airports since 2008, and that the network includes Nicosia, Larnaca, Ayia Napa, Protaras and Paphos. A standard ticket allows hand luggage and one large bag of up to 20 kg. A small pet in a crate approved for an aircraft travels free. A ticket can be bought online up to 60 minutes before departure, and changed or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before. Kapnos says passengers can park free at its Nicosia shuttle station.

The fare for the day you travel is on the Kapnos routes page. In its own articles of June 2026 the company named an adult Nicosia–Larnaca fare of €9 and a one-way Larnaca–Ayia Napa fare of €13. Those are examples from those pages, not a promise of today’s price. On the Ayia Napa route Kapnos says the Larnaca pickup is a single stop outside the terminal, and the drop-off is Ayia Napa Square (Arch. Makariou III 19) or 80 Nissi Avenue. A standard ticket does not go to the hotel door. Door-to-door, the company says, is a private or charter booking.

Limassol Airport Express

The direct Limassol bus to both airports is Limassol Airport Express, run by the Limassol urban bus company EAL Ltd. The company says on its site that it sells the tickets itself and not through other websites. An adult ticket is €10. A child aged 3 to 12 is €5. A Larnaca–Paphos connection is €15 a person. Departures in Limassol are from Agios Georgios Havouzas station. The Larnaca route also stops in the tourist area opposite St Raphael, 15 minutes after Havouzas. Tickets are bought at the station or at the airport desk.

The timetable the site marks as valid from 25 October 2026 runs the Larnaca service every day and the Paphos service on all seven days, with fewer trips on some days. The latest Larnaca airport departure on that table is 00:00, which the operator says leaves just after midnight. The same page says the times follow the flights and change when the flights change. Check the table for the date you travel, not a clipping from an article. At Christmas, New Year and Easter the company asks you to call and confirm.

The local public bus

For Larnaca airport and the Larnaca region, Hermes names Cyprus Public Transport, telephone 1416. For Paphos airport and the Paphos region it names Pafos Transport Organisation Ltd. That is local transport at the airport, not the intercity shuttle to Limassol, Nicosia or Ayia Napa. The interval and the last bus are on that operator’s own page. Kapnos writes that intercity public buses thin out after 21:00 and should not be treated as an overnight connection. That is Kapnos’s account, not a ministry timetable.

Hiring a car

Hire desks are at both airports. The daily rate is the hire company’s, not this article’s. A car is the option when there are several stops and someone in the party is prepared to drive after a night flight. The hire terms, the excess and whether the car may leave the Republic are in the contract, and they should be read before the keys are taken.

Night, children and bags

Hermes describes the taxis outside arrivals as a 24-hour service. The Limassol bus from Larnaca reaches midnight on the current table, which is not a substitute for checking the day you land. On the Limassol Airport Express the child fare is for ages 3 to 12. Luggage is included in the fixed taxi fare, according to the text of the 2021 notice. Kapnos’s standard ticket is hand luggage and one bag of up to 20 kg. A child seat, if you need one, is a question for the carrier before you pay: neither Hermes nor these bus pages puts a seat inside the fare.

Where you meet

Taxis: to the right as you leave the arrivals hall, level 0. A shuttle: the boarding point is what the operator prints on the ticket and on the airport diagram. On the Larnaca–Ayia Napa route Kapnos describes one stop outside the terminal. A private transfer with a name board looks for you in the arrivals hall. The name on the board and the flight number belong in the booking before the aircraft takes off.

Questions readers ask

Are taxis available at night at Larnaca and Paphos?

Hermes Airports says the taxis at the arrivals hall on level 0 run 24 hours at both airports.

What does a licensed airport taxi cost?

Fixed fares are set by notice K.D.P. 79/2021, which Hermes publishes. For up to four passengers, Larnaca to Limassol is €55 by day and €65 at night, and Larnaca to Nicosia is €50 and €55. The fare includes VAT and luggage. The full table is the Hermes document.

Is there a direct bus between Limassol and the airports?

Yes. Limassol Airport Express, run by EAL Ltd, serves Larnaca every day and Paphos on all seven days. The company site lists an adult ticket at €10 and a child aged 3 to 12 at €5. Times follow the flights and are published by the operator.

Does Kapnos drop you at the hotel door?

On a standard Ayia Napa ticket Kapnos drops at the square or on Nissi Avenue. The company says a specific address is a private or charter booking, not the standard ticket.
