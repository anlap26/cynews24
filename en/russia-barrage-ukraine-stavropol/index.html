Russian missiles and drones kill at least three in Ukraine; Kyiv hits Stavropol plants

Air defences reported 87 targets shot down or disabled overnight. Ukrainian drones struck a chemical works and a power station in Russia's Stavropol region. Moscow's own claims were not independently checked.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 26 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 26 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Russian forces hit homes and a school in Kharkiv overnight into Thursday, killing at least three people, with others possibly still under rubble. The wider attack mixed ballistic missiles and drones and also caused casualties in Kyiv and other regions. Euronews, citing Ukrainian reports, said the salvo included Iskander-M, Zircon and S-400 missiles plus 113 Shahed, Gerbera and Parodiya decoy drones, 76 of them jet-powered. Air defences shot down or disabled 87 aerial targets.

Two people were killed when a strike hit a residential building in Kharkiv. A drone hit a block in the Novobavarsky district and injured three, and rescuers said more people might be trapped. A third person was killed and several were wounded at an educational building in the city's Kyiv district. Seven people were hurt in a missile strike on the capital. One person was wounded in Dnipropetrovsk region. In Donetsk region, a strike on a post office in Bylbasivka on Tuesday evening killed one person and injured three.

The same night, Ukrainian drones hit a thermal power plant and the Nevinnomyssky Azot chemical plant in Nevinnomyssk, in Russia's Stavropol region. The plant, part of EuroChem, makes ammonium nitrate, ammonia and acetic acid, and Euronews reported that components from the site go into the explosives octogen and hexogen. It has been attacked at least six times since the full-scale invasion began in February 2022. The neighbouring Nevinnomysskaya power station, which supplies the plant, was also hit. Regional authorities said production and energy sites were damaged, along with a school and a university branch, and that two people were wounded. Schools in the town moved online. A new fire was reported at the Kuibyshev refinery in Samara region, a day after strikes on that plant and the nearby Syzran refinery. Ukraine also hit the Subkhankulovo pipeline station in Bashkortostan, a hub on the Transneft system. Russia's defence ministry said 343 Ukrainian drones were destroyed over 15 regions, the Azov and Black Seas and occupied Crimea. Euronews said it could not verify that claim on its own.
