Cyprus weather by month: the pattern, not a forecast

Summers are hot and almost dry. Most rain falls from autumn into early spring. The Department of Meteorology publishes the normals. A city forecast is not this page, and the coast is not the mountains.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Cyprus is talked about as endless summer. The Department of Meteorology's own rainfall normals are less romantic and more useful. They describe the government-controlled area as a place with a wet half of the year and a dry half, not as a single temperature you can pack for in every town.

The rain, from the Department's table

The Department's monthly rainfall bulletin prints a normal, an average for 1961 to 1990, beside the actual rain. For the area under government control those normals, in millimetres, are October 32.7, November 53.3, December 105.6, January 102.4, February 81.6, March 61.9, April 29.9, May 19.6, June 6.0, July 2.6, August 2.9, September 4.5. The annual normal on that sheet is 503.0. The sheet says the figures are area averages. They are not the rain that fell on one hotel, and they are not a forecast. The same PDF shows the actual month next to the normal, and in a dry year the actual is a long way under the line. Read the latest bulletin if you need this season rather than the climate.

What the shape says is simple. The heavy months are December and January. November through March carry most of the year's water. June, July and August are almost dry on the normal. April and May, and then September and October, are the transitions. A week of cloud in August does not make August a rainy month on this table. A sunny week in January does not make January dry.

Coast, plain, mountain

The Department publishes station data as well as the island average. This guide does not copy a station's July high from a third-party table. The pattern the stations are there to show is the one residents already live: the coast is cooled by the sea, the central plain around Nicosia is hotter on summer afternoons and colder on winter nights, and Troodos, at mountain height, is a different climate. Snow on the Troodos roads in winter is ordinary enough that a coastal packing list is the wrong list for the mountains. If you need a number for a town, open the Department's climatological page and the station sheet, and keep the date of the normal in mind. A 1961-1990 rain normal and a later temperature normal are not the same object.

Sea temperature is not in the rainfall bulletin. People swim for a long season on the coast. This page will not invent a degree for the water in May or in November. A beach flag and a current report beat a guide that guessed.

How to use the year

If you are timing a holiday, the dry heat runs from late spring into early autumn, and the swimmable sea is a coastal fact rather than a mountain one. If you are timing a move, rain and heat are costs: damp in a winter flat, air conditioning in July, and a different bill in each case. Neither is priced here. Renting and the cost of living refuse a single figure for the same reason this page refuses a single temperature.

Beaches people ask about by name are listed, without a ranking, in beaches in Cyprus. Flights into the Republic's airports are in flights to Cyprus. A search is not a forecast.

Questions readers ask

When does it rain?

The Department's area normals for 1961 to 1990, printed on its monthly rainfall bulletin, put most of the rain between October and March. June, July and August are the dry months on that table. A single storm does not repeal the normal.

Is the whole island the same temperature?

No. The coast is milder. Nicosia and the plain run hotter in summer and colder on winter nights. Troodos is a mountain climate, and snow falls there in winter. The Department publishes station sheets. This page does not invent a degree for a beach.

When can you swim?

The sea is warmest in late summer and cooler in winter. This page does not print a sea temperature, because it is not in the bulletin cited here. Check the Department or a current coastal report before you plan a swim.

What is a normal, as opposed to this year?

A normal is a long average, here the 1961-1990 area average the Department still prints beside each month's actual rain. The actual month can be far from it. The bulletin shows both.

Does this page say the best month to move?

No. Weather is not a residence rule. Flights and the two Republic airports are in the flights guide. A milder month is still a month you insure and pack for.
