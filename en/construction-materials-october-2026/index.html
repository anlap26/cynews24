Cyprus construction material prices rose 4.09% in October 2026

Construction materials Cyprus October 2026: the index hit 123.90, base 2021, up 0.39% on the month and 4.09% on the year. Electrical fixtures rose 9.54%, the steepest line in the release.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 18 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 18 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

The price index of construction materials reached 123.90 units in October 2026, with 2021 set at 100, the statistical service said on Wednesday. It rose 0.39 per cent from September and 4.09 per cent from October 2025. For January to October it was 2.43 per cent higher than the same months of 2025. Prices are collected excluding VAT, the reference date is the 15th, and the index tracks what materials cost the contractor, not the sale price of a finished home.

By main category the service recorded annual rises in metallic products of 6.18 per cent, in wood, insulation materials, chemicals and plastics of 5.21 per cent, in minerals of 3.26 per cent, in electromechanical products of 3.01 per cent and in mineral products of 1.81 per cent.

Metallic products reached 112.13, up 0.36 per cent on the month and 3.16 per cent over the ten months. Inside that group, aluminium and other metal products rose 6.27 per cent on the year, to 124.11, and iron and steel products rose 6.13 per cent, to 106.84.

Wood, insulation, chemicals and plastics stood at 126.61. Plastic products, at 132.48, were unchanged on the month and 6.43 per cent higher than a year earlier. Wood products rose 5.59 per cent on the year. Chemical products rose 4.11 per cent. Insulation materials fell 0.05 per cent on the month and were still 2.85 per cent above October 2025.

Electromechanical products posted the largest monthly rise among the main categories, 0.67 per cent, to 120.85. Their annual increase was 3.01 per cent. Electrical fixtures were the steepest line in the release, up 9.54 per cent on the year to 145.44 after a 1.29 per cent monthly rise. Heating and cooling equipment was unchanged on the month at 94.08 and 7.18 per cent cheaper than in October 2025.

Minerals reached 140.31, up 0.56 per cent on the month. Cement, at 146.10, rose 0.85 per cent on the month and 5.45 per cent on the year. Mineral products, a separate category, reached 128.52, up 1.81 per cent on the year.

Cyprus Mail, reporting the same release, said the annual rate had been 3.74 per cent in September and 3.39 per cent in August. October’s consumer-price index is a different measure, and it is here. The construction index does not say what a finished house costs.
