Jobs and pay in Cyprus: what is published, and who is allowed to work

Cystat publishes earnings statistics. It does not publish a salary for your offer. Social insurance, in PwC's 2026 summary, is 8.8 percent from the employee and 8.8 percent from the employer, up to a ceiling. A right to work depends on the passport.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

A job search that starts with "salaries in Cyprus" wants one number. The Statistical Service publishes earnings statistics by release, not a wage for the offer in your inbox. This guide points at the releases and then at the deductions that a legal job actually carries. It does not rank employers and it does not invent a monthly pay for a developer, a nurse or a waiter.

Earnings, and the minimum

Cystat is the office that publishes average earnings. Use the latest release, and read which population it covers. An average for an entire economy is not the pay in one sector, and a sector average is not your contract. The Ministry of Labour and Social Insurance publishes the national minimum wage by decree. Decrees are replaced. A figure copied into a guide becomes the previous decree the week the Ministry signs a new one. That is why this page refuses the number and sends you to mlsi.gov.cy. If an employer quotes the minimum, ask to see the decree they are using.

The cost of living guide explains why that wage, even once you have it, is not a budget. Rent is a separate contract.

What a payslip is made of

PwC's Tax Facts and Figures 2026 states social-insurance contributions at 8.8 percent for the employee and 8.8 percent for the employer, applied up to a maximum. The maximum level of emoluments for 2026, in that booklet, is €68,904, which it also states as €1,325 a week and €5,742 a month. The same booklet says the 8.8 percent rate applies from 1 January 2024 for five years. It lists further employer contributions: a social cohesion fund at 2 percent with no maximum, a redundancy fund at 1.2 percent, an industrial training fund at 0.5 percent, and a holiday fund at 8 percent where the employer is not exempt. Those are PwC's statements of the funds. The Social Insurance Services are the collector. A payslip that shows none of this, paid in cash with a promise to "sort the stamps later", is not the scheme the booklet describes.

GeSY sits on top for people in the categories that pay it. The Health Insurance Organisation's rates, 2.65 percent from the employee and 2.90 percent from the employer, are in GeSY for foreigners. Income tax is a third line, and only if the person is taxable. Bands and residence are in tax residency. A first-job exemption of 50 percent, where PwC describes it, has conditions. They are not applied by the fact of moving. See the non-dom guide and then the statute.

Who is allowed to take the job

An EU, EEA or Swiss citizen who registers may work. The registration is in EU residence in Cyprus. A third-country visitor's permit is not a work permit. The site's relocation guide says so directly. The digital nomad scheme is remote work for an employer or clients abroad, and it forbids Cyprus clients. It is not a local job. The nomad guide has the Department's wording.

A cap on how long some third-country workers may stay was reported as news, not as a wage. The report is the four-year cap. Read it if it might apply to the permit you are being offered. A job in a licensed financial firm is not a job in a café. Fintech jobs and registrations is the news item. CySEC is the licence, which a job offer does not replace.

Nothing here is a vacancy. If the pay is only a headline, ask for the payslip lines before you move.

Questions readers ask

What is the average salary?

Cystat publishes earnings releases. This page does not copy a figure from a table it has not reproduced, because the next release replaces it. Read the current Cystat table for the occupation you mean.

What is the minimum wage?

The Ministry of Labour sets it by decree. The number changes. This page does not print a euro amount that might already be the previous decree. Read the Ministry's current text before you sign.

What comes off a legal payslip?

PwC's 2026 booklet states social insurance at 8.8 percent for the employee and 8.8 percent for the employer, on earnings up to €68,904 in 2026. GeSY adds 2.65 percent for the employee and 2.90 percent for the employer, on the HIO's page. Other employer funds exist. A cash wage with none of these is not the legal payslip.

Can I work on a visitor permit or a nomad permit?

No. The relocation guide says a pink slip is not permission to work. The nomad scheme forbids work for a Cyprus employer or a Cyprus client. EU citizens use the registration route and may work. A third-country job needs its own permit.

Is a 50 percent tax exemption automatic?

No. PwC describes conditions for a first-employment exemption. They are summarised in the non-dom guide as conditions, not as a result. Do not let an employer deduct it before you have read the text.
