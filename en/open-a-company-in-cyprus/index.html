Opening a company in Cyprus in 2026: the form, and the 15 percent rate

The usual vehicle is a private company limited by shares: at least one shareholder, no more than fifty, and no offer of shares to the public. The Registrar's fee for a company with share capital is €165. From 1 January 2026 the corporate tax rate in PwC's summary is 15 percent, not 12.5.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

A Cyprus company is a file at the Registrar of Companies, plus a tax life that is not the same file. The pages used here are the Registrar's own description of a private company limited by shares, the Registrar's page on applying to incorporate, and PwC's Tax Facts and Figures 2026 where a rate has to be stated. PwC is a summary. The statute wins if they diverge.

The form most people mean

The Registrar says a private company limited by shares has a share capital, and the liability of its members is limited by the memorandum to any unpaid amount on the shares they hold. It must have at least one shareholder and no more than fifty, not counting people who are or were employees and who are members. It cannot offer its shares to the public. The Registrar calls this the most common type. Other types exist. This page does not describe a public company, a partnership or a branch, because those are different filings. A foreign company that only opens a place of business has a notification duty of its own on the Registrar's site. Read that page if you are not incorporating.

What the Registrar asks for

Name approval comes first. The incorporation page then says you may file online or by hand. The online list includes a statutory declaration, form HE1, signed and sworn before the court by the entrusted lawyer; the memorandum and articles, duly signed, in Greek; a declaration of the witness to the signatures; and, if you want a file of translations, a certified translation. The fee stated on that page is €165 for a company with share capital and €235 for a company without. Payment is by card for the online route, in the Registrar's wording. A lawyer's bill, a registered office and a secretary are not inside the €165. This guide does not invent them. The Registrar's company-type page does not, in the text used here, print a minimum share capital. Do not take a figure from a forum and call it the Registrar's.

At least one director and a secretary are part of how these companies are actually run. The Chambers practice note and the Registrar's lifecycle pages are the place to confirm the current minimum before you appoint yourself to every seat. This guide will not state a number of directors it has not copied from the Registrar's type page, and that page, in the text above, speaks about shareholders.

Tax, from 2026

PwC's 2026 booklet says corporation tax was 12.5 percent through 31 December 2025 and is 15 percent from 1 January 2026. It also says that from 2026 a company incorporated in Cyprus is, by default, tax resident in Cyprus, unless a double-tax treaty provides otherwise. Companies that moved their seat to Cyprus count as incorporated for that test, in PwC's wording. A company that is tax resident is taxed on income from Cyprus and abroad, with the exemptions the law lists. Dividends received by a company are, in the ordinary case PwC describes, exempt, with exceptions it prints. Interest received by a company is, from 1 January 2026, within corporation tax rather than the old split between corporation tax and defence contribution, again in that booklet.

None of those sentences is a computation. A director's salary is the director's income tax, which is tax residency if the person is resident, and it is not the company's 15 percent. Non-dom status is personal, in the non-dom guide. It does not attach to the company.

Crypto gains are a special line in the same PwC booklet: from 1 January 2026, gains from cryptocurrency transactions are taxed at 8 percent, and that mode does not apply to gains on crypto acquired through mining. Whether a given token is that kind of gain is not decided here. The regulatory side, which is a different question from the tax, is CySEC, forex and crypto.

What incorporation does not do

It does not give you a residence permit. The immigration routes are in EU residence in Cyprus. It does not open a bank account. Banks ask who owns the company, which is opening an account. It does not authorise you to offer investment services. That is CySEC, not the Registrar.

This page is not a completed HE1 and not a tax opinion.

Questions readers ask

What sort of company is the common one?

The Registrar describes a private company limited by shares. It needs at least one shareholder and no more than fifty, not counting employees who are members. It cannot offer its shares to the public. Liability is limited to any unpaid amount on the shares.

What does incorporation cost at the Registrar?

The Registrar's incorporation page states €165 for a company with share capital and €235 for a company without share capital. That is the Registrar's fee in that text. It is not a lawyer's fee, and this page does not invent one.

Does the memorandum have to be in Greek?

The Registrar says the memorandum and articles are submitted in Greek. A certified translation can be filed if you want a translation file for later certified copies. The page also requires a statutory declaration, form HE1, sworn before the court by the entrusted lawyer.

What is the corporate tax rate in 2026?

PwC's Tax Facts and Figures 2026 says the rate was 12.5 percent up to 31 December 2025 and is 15 percent from 1 January 2026. PwC also says that from 2026 a company incorporated in Cyprus is tax resident by default unless a treaty treats it as resident elsewhere. Confirm both points against the statute if you are filing.

Does a company give me the right to live in Cyprus?

No. Incorporation is a Registrar filing. Residence is a Migration Department question, covered in the EU residence guide. A bank account for the company is a third decision, covered in the banking guide.
