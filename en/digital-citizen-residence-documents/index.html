Digital Citizen now stores foreign residents’ residence documents

EU and third-country nationals in Cyprus can add a residence document to Digital Citizen, and children’s Cypriot ID cards can be added too. Originals are still needed to travel.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 18 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 18 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Foreign residents can now keep their Cyprus residence document inside the Digital Citizen app, and Cypriot identity cards of children under 18 can be added as well, the deputy ministry for research, innovation and digital policy said on Tuesday. Phileleftheros and the Cyprus Mail both reported the change. Vestnik Kipra carried it on Wednesday and added how the copies may be used.

Cypriot users could already store their own identity card, a driving licence, a fan card and an MOT certificate. They can now add the Cypriot identity cards of their children under 18. EU citizens and third-country nationals living in Cyprus can add their residence document. They can also add a child’s Cypriot identity card where the child has one, both papers reported.

The app warns users when a document is close to expiry, so there is time to arrange a renewal. Once a new document has been issued, the expired digital copy can be replaced in the app. The ministry described the additions as part of a wider effort to make dealings with the state simpler and to offer digital services that are useful day to day.

Vestnik Kipra, drawing on Reporter, Gov.cy and Phileleftheros, says signing in needs a CY Login account. A foreign resident can create one with an ARC number, the alien registration number, and the residence document. The site says documents in the app have the same force as the paper versions when they are shown inside Cyprus. For travel abroad, the originals are still required. It also says an expired digital document stays visible until the user replaces it or deletes it.

The same report says the migration department, on 10 November, announced a separate notification service. It sends SMS and email reminders when a residence document is nearing expiry, and it reports how an application is progressing. To receive those messages, Vestnik writes, a person signs in with a verified CY Login and registers contact details through the state service “Update my personal data”.

A residence file and a login are different problems. The steps for a new arrival, including the yellow slip, the pink slip and GeSY, are set out in the resident handbook. How to protect the account that holds those logins, including CY Login and a bank, is in the guide on data and foreign services.
