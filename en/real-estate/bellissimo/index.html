Villa

Bellissimo Villa

€955,600 + VAT

Request info

Paphos, Chloraka · 3 bedrooms · 209.5 m² · Available

Bellissimo Villa is a project by ATEX Developers Ltd in Chloraka, west Paphos: four exclusive villas in two types, with sea views. Two of the four villas are still available. Each available villa has a 550 m² plot. The covered area is 209.5 m²: 147 m² internal, 44 m² of covered verandas and 18.5 m² of covered parking, plus a 31 m² uncovered veranda. The energy rating is A.

Each villa has three bedrooms, three bathrooms and a separate WC, an open-plan kitchen and living room, a private swimming pool, a private garden and terraces. The windows are double-glazed aluminium, and the finishes are high quality.

The beach is about 300 m away, and a Blue Flag beach is a five-minute drive. A supermarket is 50 m away. Paphos centre is five minutes away and Paphos airport about 20 minutes. The price is €955,600 per villa, excluding VAT. Coordinates: 34.7991111, 32.4006667.
