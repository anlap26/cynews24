EU residence in Cyprus: the routes, and the clocks they actually use

There is no statute called the first 90 days. A Union citizen who stays applies for MEU1 within four months. A third-country visitor applies before the visa runs out. A digital nomad file is a third route. None of these is tax residence.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

People arriving in Cyprus ask for a residence checklist as if one form covered every passport. The Migration Department runs several. The site's relocation guide already puts the first months in the order the deadlines impose, and it points at MyCyTax for the underlying pages. This guide is the map of those routes. Where it repeats a fee or a deadline, it is repeating that guide and the Department, not discovering a new number. If the Department's page shows a different fee on the day you file, the Department is right.

Union citizens

A citizen of the European Union, the European Economic Area or Switzerland who will stay uses the registration certificate known as MEU1, the yellow slip. The relocation guide says the application is made within four months of entry, that the Department's fee is €20, and that the certificate does not expire. The holder may work. A permanent right comes later, on the terms the free-movement law sets, not on the day of the first certificate. This page does not calculate the five years for you.

The papers a later form asks for often include a place to live. Renting in Cyprus is the tenancy page. Buying is a different permission for some nationalities, even when the buyer is not the person who needs MEU1. See buying as a foreigner before you assume an EU passport removes every property rule. The Ministry's current page frames the property permit around non-EU citizens. Read it anyway if a company or a spouse is part of the purchase.

Third-country visitors

A third-country national who is visiting and not working uses the permit the relocation guide calls MVIS8, the pink slip, and files before the visa expires. The guide is plain that this permit is not permission to work and not permission to carry out an economic activity. Employment is a different file. An investment permit under Regulation 6(2), tied to criteria the Department publishes, is a third route again. It is not citizenship and it is not tax residence. A checklist for the yellow slip will not produce the pink slip. An investment file is not examined as a visitor application.

The nomad scheme, only if it is actually your route

Non-EU and non-EEA nationals who work remotely for an employer or clients abroad, and who meet an income test, have a separate scheme. The cap, the €3,500 net figure, the family rule and the ban on Cyprus clients are in the digital nomad guide. Filing a nomad application on a Union form, or a Union application on a nomad form, wastes the weeks the Department says an examination takes.

What none of the cards are

They are not tax residence. The tests are in Cyprus tax residency in 2026. They are not GeSY enrolment. The categories are in GeSY for foreigners. They are not a driving licence. The 185-day exchange rule is in driving in Cyprus. They are not a bank account. That decision is the bank's, in opening an account.

A Schengen stamp is not one of these cards either, because Cyprus is not in Schengen. See Cyprus and Schengen in 2026 before you plan the rest of a European year around a Larnaca landing.

The Department's address for the current form is gov.cy under the Deputy Ministry of Migration. Take the form from there, not from a screenshot in a chat.

Questions readers ask

What is the yellow slip?

It is the registration certificate, MEU1, for a citizen of the EU, the EEA or Switzerland who stays. The site's relocation guide says the application is within four months of entry and that the Migration Department's fee is €20. Check the Department if you are filing, in case the fee has moved.

What is the pink slip?

It is the visitor permit, MVIS8 in the relocation guide's wording, for a third-country national who is not working. It has to be filed before the visa expires. It is not permission to work and it is not the nomad scheme.

Can EU citizens work?

Yes, once they are on the registration route the relocation guide describes. The certificate does not expire. A third-country national needs a permit that matches the work. A visitor file will not be examined as a work file.

Where does the digital nomad visa fit?

It is a separate scheme for non-EU, non-EEA remote workers, with its own income test and a ban on Cyprus clients. The detail is in the nomad guide. Do not file it on a Union-citizen form.

Does the card make me tax resident or a GeSY patient?

No. Tax residence is the 183-day or 60-day test. GeSY has its own beneficiary categories. Both are linked. A card is evidence of an immigration status only.
