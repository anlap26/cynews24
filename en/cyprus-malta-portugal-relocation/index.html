Cyprus, Malta or Portugal for a move: a comparison that refuses a winner

All three are in the European Union. Malta and Portugal are in Schengen. Cyprus is not. The tax nicknames are not the same regime. Income tests sit on each government's own page and are not copied here.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

People comparing Cyprus, Malta and Portugal are usually comparing a life, then discovering that the advertisements compared a tax nickname. This page keeps the three countries in one view and refuses to score them. The facts that are stable enough to print are memberships, languages and the side of the road. The facts that move, visas and tax incentives, are named by the office that publishes them.

What does not depend on a brochure

All three are members of the European Union. A Union citizen's right to move is not the same as a third-country national's permit, in any of them. Malta and Portugal are in the Schengen area. Cyprus is not. The state of the Cypriot application, including the sentence about 1 July 2027, is in Cyprus and Schengen in 2026. If free movement inside Schengen after you land is the point of the move, that difference is the one to put at the top. If it is not, do not let it decide the rest.

Cyprus and Malta drive on the left. Portugal drives on the right. That is a daily fact, not a curiosity, if you are bringing a car or a habit. Official languages: Greek and Turkish in the Republic of Cyprus, with English widely used in business and on the coast; English and Maltese in Malta; Portuguese in Portugal. A school, a hospital form and a tenancy follow the official language more often than a restaurant menu does.

The permits people mean

For a non-EU remote worker, Cyprus publishes a digital nomad scheme. The income test, the cap of 500 and the ban on working for Cyprus clients are in the digital nomad guide, taken from the Migration Department. Malta's Residency Malta Agency publishes a nomad residence permit of its own. The income figure and the length are on that site. They are not reprinted here, because a copied threshold is how a comparison goes stale. Portugal's entry for third-country nationals runs through AIMA. The residence people call D8, for remote work, is a Portuguese file. Read AIMA for the current income test and the documents. This page will not invent a Portuguese minimum in order to put three numbers in a row.

EU citizens are in a different queue in each country. The Cypriot one is summarised in EU residence in Cyprus. Do not hand a Cypriot checklist to a Portuguese office.

The tax nicknames

Cyprus non-dom status is an exemption from Special Defence Contribution on dividends and interest for a person who is tax resident and not domiciled. It is not a discount on salary. The 2026 shape of the test is in the non-dom guide. Corporate tax is a company question, and from 2026 the rate summarised by PwC is 15 percent, which is opening a company.

Malta's tax system has its own residence and remittance features. They are not "Cyprus non-dom with a different flag". Read the Maltese tax authority rather than a relocation chart.

Portugal's non-habitual resident regime was a specific incentive. It is not, for a new applicant in 2026, the product that was advertised for a decade. A different incentive, IFICI, is aimed at defined activities. Whether a given job is on that list is a question for the Portuguese tax authority, not for a headline that says Portugal is finished as a tax destination or that it replaced NHR with the same thing. This guide marks that as uncertain for any reader who has not opened the current Portuguese notice.

Money you will actually spend

None of the three governments publishes one monthly cost of a life in a named city that this page could honestly copy. The Cypriot version of that refusal is the cost of living guide. A warmer winter is not a lower rent. Weather by month is climate, not a reason to skip a lease.

Search flights to Cyprus

A flight search is not a decision. The decision is which office's form matches the passport and the work.

Questions readers ask

Which one is best?

This page does not say. A remote worker, a retiree and a family with a school-age child are not choosing the same thing. The pages below are the official ones to read for each country.

Which are in Schengen?

Malta and Portugal apply Schengen. Cyprus does not, as of this guide's update on 25 November 2026. A reported 2027 date is not a decision. See the Cyprus Schengen guide.

Is the old Portuguese NHR still open?

The non-habitual resident regime in the form used before 2024 is not a door this guide tells new applicants to walk through. Portugal now points specific activities at a different incentive, often called IFICI. Read the Portuguese tax authority. Do not trust a blog that still advertises the old NHR as open.

Does Cyprus non-dom exist in Malta and Portugal under the same name?

No. Cyprus non-dom is a Special Defence Contribution status described on the Cyprus Tax Department page. Malta has its own residence and tax rules on Maltese government sites. They are not interchangeable labels.

Where is the income figure for a nomad permit?

For Cyprus, the Migration Department prints €3,500 net a month and a cap of 500, in the digital nomad guide. For Malta, read the Residency Malta Agency. For Portugal, read AIMA. This page does not copy a Maltese or Portuguese euro figure it has not set beside the live page.
