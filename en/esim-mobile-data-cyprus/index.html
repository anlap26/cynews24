eSIM and mobile data in Cyprus for tourists

eSIM in Cyprus for tourists: the Republic is in the EU, so an EU subscriber is under roam-like-at-home. A separate plan is for a non-EU home SIM. This page does not invent local SIM prices.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 19 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 19 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

The Republic of Cyprus is a member of the European Union. A customer of an operator in the EU or the EEA, while inside the Republic, is under roam like at home: calls, texts and data follow the home plan, within the fair-use rules Your Europe describes. That is not a shop discount and it is not an unlimited-forever bundle. If the home operator is not in the EU or the EEA, that rule does not by itself cover a Cypriot network, and the choice is a local SIM or a travel eSIM.

Your Europe says this roaming regime is the EU and the EEA. This page does not extend it to the north of the island, where the networks are different. The Deputy Ministry of Tourism’s guide speaks about the Republic, its airports at Larnaca and Paphos, and its resorts. Check coverage with your own operator before a trip north. Do not take it from this article.

What to install before the flight

A phone that accepts an eSIM can be set up before boarding. Then the arrivals hall at Larnaca or Paphos already has data, without a queue at a shop. A travel eSIM is often data, while ordinary calls and texts stay on the home SIM or move to apps. That is how many international eSIMs are built. The gigabytes and the validity are on the seller’s page the day you buy. This article does not copy them: price lists change, and the state does not publish one official “eSIM for Cyprus” rate.

Airalo is the main travel-eSIM link on this page. Yesim is the alternative. Neither button is a “Cyprus for 7 days” price: the gigabytes are on the seller’s page the day you buy.

A local SIM after you land

A physical card is bought on the island, in an operator’s shop. The Republic does not print one tourist tariff in the tourism booklet, so this page names neither a euro-per-gigabyte figure nor a “100 GB for a fortnight”. Read the operator’s site, or the price in the shop, on the day. A shop may ask for a passport. If the counter does not, do not assume every shop asks.

EU roaming does not make a local card pointless. A local card is for a home operator outside the EU, for a small home allowance, or for a Cypriot number and ordinary calls rather than data alone.

What this page does not promise

It does not promise an eSIM on every phone: that depends on the model and on whether the handset is locked. It does not promise a signal in every Troodos valley. And it does not replace medical insurance. A policy for the trip is a different product; the breakdown without prices is in the health-insurance guide. Questions readers ask

Does EU roaming work in Cyprus?

Your Europe describes roam like at home for EU and EEA subscribers inside the EU. The Republic of Cyprus is an EU member. This page does not extend that regime to the north of the island.

How much is a tourist eSIM?

There is no single official price. Gigabytes and validity are on the seller’s page the day you buy. The button on this page opens the Airalo homepage, not a plan.

Do I need a local SIM if I already have an EU SIM?

Not in order to switch roaming on. It helps if you want a Cypriot number or the home allowance is small. If the operator is outside the EU and the EEA, roam like at home does not apply by itself.
