Eden Coast, Geroskipou: what bbf publishes about the Paphos project

An independent reading of :eden coast. This is not a developer listing and not a partnership sale. The official page gives a €940,000 start and 16 homes, and it states the sea as both 50 m and 20 metres.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 20 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 20 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

This is an independent reading of public material on :eden coast in Geroskipou, Paphos. CyNews 24 is not the developer bbf and is not selling these homes. The figures below are the developer’s or an agent’s. They can change. VAT is extra where a source says so.

On its project page, bbf calls :eden coast an exclusive beachfront development in Paphos. The summary line says 50 m to the sea, a starting price of €940,000, 16 units and energy class A+. The same page’s text places the project “just 20 metres” from a Blue Flag beach on the shore of Geroskipou. Those two distances sit on one page. A brochure the developer publishes also says “twenty meters” from a Blue Flag beach.

That PDF sets out the mix. Blocks A, E and M each have four homes: ground-floor two-bedroom apartments with an interior of 102.70 m², and first-floor three-bedroom penthouses of 119.80 m² with a private rooftop pool. There are four villas, each with its own pool: H31 has four bedrooms and 239.60 m² of interior, and H32, H33 and H34 have three bedrooms and 171.50 m². That is 16 homes, matching the project page.

The project page promises indoor and outdoor pools, a fitness centre, a spa, co-working and gardens. Its amenity list also names a sauna, a meeting room, a kids’ club and a leisure terrace. It does not use the name “Central Gardens”, and it does not give pool lengths of 25 m and 18 m, ceiling heights of 3.10 m, or underfloor heating. Prime Property Group publishes those details and places the amenities in “Central Gardens”, including a 145 m² gym. That is an agent’s text, not a line on bbf’s page.

The €940,000 start is the figure on bbf’s page. The page does not say whether VAT is included. Lion & Land describes €940,000 as a price before VAT and gives areas of about 102.70 to 239.60 m². Prime Property publishes these asking figures: a two-bedroom apartment at €940,000, a three-bedroom penthouse at €1,090,000, a three-bedroom villa at €1,470,000 and a four-bedroom villa at €2,140,000. Antaria Homes gives a range of 103–240 m² and a handover in the fourth quarter of 2029. Prime says October 2029, and Lion & Land says October 2029 as well. The project page we read does not state a completion date.

What the published record supports: a small scheme, energy class A+, and a seafront claim made by the developer, with a PDF that itemises the apartments and villas. What it does not settle: the distance to the sea disagrees on the official page, the starting price does not explain VAT, and the handover date is on agent pages rather than the project page.

Before any contract, the schedule of areas matters more than a “from” price. PwC’s Cyprus summary puts the standard VAT rate at 19%. It describes the 5% rate as applying to the first 130 m² of buildable area, up to a value of €350,000, if the total buildable area does not exceed 190 m², the transaction value does not exceed €475,000, and the home is the main residence for ten years. The published “from €940,000” is above that transaction ceiling. This is not tax advice: the thresholds belong with the Tax Department. The Department of Lands and Surveys says a transfer that is subject to VAT is fully exempt from transfer fees, and a transfer that is not subject to VAT gets a 50% reduction of those fees. The Tax Department imposes capital gains tax at 20% on a profit from disposing of immovable property in Cyprus, with listed exemptions.

Questions readers ask

Does the €940,000 starting price include VAT?

bbf’s project page states a starting price of €940,000 and does not say whether VAT is included. Lion & Land describes that figure as a price before VAT.

How far is Eden Coast from the sea?

The bbf page says 50 m in its summary and “just 20 metres” from a Blue Flag beach in the text. The developer PDF also says twenty meters. The figures do not match.

When is completion?

The project page we read does not give a date. Antaria Homes says the fourth quarter of 2029. Prime Property and Lion & Land say October 2029.
