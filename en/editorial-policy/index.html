Editorial policy and corrections

Accuracy

We publish facts that appear in the sources we cite. We do not invent quotes, statistics or events. If sources disagree, we say so or we do not treat the point as settled.

Sourcing

Each news article lists its sources and links to them. The article is a rewritten summary, not a copy of those reports. Readers should open the links for the full original. Citing an outlet is not an endorsement, and it does not mean that outlet wrote our text.

Corrections

If you believe a story is wrong, email cynews24@gmail.com with the article URL, the passage and the correction. We review the request. When a correction is needed, we change the article and its updated time. A correction is not removed because it is inconvenient.

Advertising is separate from news

Sponsored or paid material is labelled and kept separate from newsroom reporting. Advertising enquiries go through the advertising and contact page and are not handled as news. Rates are available on request.

Contact the newsroom

Editorial questions and correction requests: cynews24@gmail.com. The publisher is Anna, and the site is based in Cyprus.
