Nicosia Christmas market returns to Eleftheria Square for a second year

From 28 November to 27 December, more than 45 local handmade makers will fill the square, with free entry.

By the CyNews 24 newsroom10 November 2026

The Nicosia Christmas Market, organised with Handmade in Cyprus, comes back to Eleftheria Square for its second year, running from 28 November to 27 December 2026.

Entry is free. Each day the market is expected to host more than 45 local makers of handmade goods, alongside festive food and drink.

The programme also includes children's crafts and entertainment organised by Nicosia municipality, which presents the event as the city's Christmas village.

For visitors planning a winter weekend in the capital, it adds a festive stop to the city's calendar; dates and details are published by the municipality and event listings.
