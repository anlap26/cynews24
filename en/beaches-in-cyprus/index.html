Beaches in Cyprus: places people ask for, not a ranking

Nissi, Fig Tree Bay, Coral Bay, Lara, Konnos and Governor's Beach are real places. A Blue Flag is an annual award, not a permanent grade. This page does not call one of them the best.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Lists of the best beaches in Cyprus are mostly the same names in a different order. The order is the part this guide will not invent. What it will do is say which places the names point at, who gives a Blue Flag, and which neighbouring pages cover the weather, the car and the flight. Nothing below is a cleanliness score. A flag on a pole is a better source than a sentence.

Names, and where they sit

Nissi is the sandy bay in Ayia Napa that appears on almost every foreign search. Fig Tree Bay and the Protaras coast, including Konnos, are the east-coast names next to it. Coral Bay is the sheltered bay north of Paphos town. Lara, further along the Paphos coast, is known for a turtle nesting beach and for being less built-up than the hotel strip. Governor's Beach is on the south coast in the Limassol district, stonier than the Ayia Napa sand. In Larnaca, Finikoudes is the town promenade and Mackenzie is the beach beside the salt lake and the airport approach. Lady's Mile runs along the Akrotiri salt lake towards Limassol. These are descriptions of where a name sits. They are not a claim that one is cleaner, safer or emptier on the day you go.

Visit Cyprus, the Republic's tourism site, publishes beach material of its own. Use it for how the place presents itself. Use your eyes for the seaweed, the wind and the jet skis.

A Blue Flag is for a season

CYMEPA is the Cyprus body associated with the Blue Flag programme. The flag is awarded for a bathing season against criteria that include water quality and how the beach is managed. The list changes. A guide that names "the Blue Flag beaches of 2026" without opening that year's list is guessing. This one does not open a ranking and does not paste an old list as if it were current. Before you choose a hotel for the flag, read CYMEPA's current page.

Water quality on a given morning is not the award. A beach can hold a flag and still be rough, or lose the flag and still be a pleasant swim. The Department of Meteorology is the climate source, in weather by month. It does not issue a beach review.

Getting there, and staying

Public buses serve some resorts better than others, and Sunday service is a question for the current timetable, not for this paragraph. Hiring a car is covered in renting a car in Cyprus, including the point that traffic keeps left. A day trip and a week in one bay are different bookings. Where to stay is the accommodation guide. Flights covers Larnaca and Paphos, which are the Republic's international airports.

Tours and tickets, where a company sells them, change price. Klook is one of the affiliate links the site is allowed to use. The button does not review a beach.

Shade, fresh water and a hat matter more in July than in November. The weather page is the reason. This page will not tell you a beach is safe for a child, empty of jellyfish or free to enter. Those are conditions of the day.

Questions readers ask

Which is the best beach?

This page does not say. Best depends on wind, the month, whether you need a bus, and whether a bay is sheltered. The names below are places readers ask about, not a league table.

What is a Blue Flag?

It is an award in a programme of the Foundation for Environmental Education. In Cyprus the partner people look up is CYMEPA. The award is for a season. A beach that had a flag last summer does not automatically have one now. Read the current list.

Are these beaches in the Republic?

The places named here are on the coast of the area visitors usually reach through Larnaca or Paphos. This page does not describe beaches in the north and does not suggest a crossing as a beach trip.

Is the sea warm all year?

No. The weather guide separates the dry summer from the wetter winter and refuses a made-up sea temperature. Swim to the conditions of the day, not to a paragraph.

Do I need a car?

Some bays have a bus in season and some do not. The car-hire guide is the practical page. A transfer from the airport is a different booking from a day at a beach.
