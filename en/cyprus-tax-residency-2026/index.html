Cyprus tax residency in 2026: the 183-day rule and the 60-day rule

A residence card does not make a person tax resident. The Tax Department uses a 183-day test or a 60-day test. From 2026 the 60-day test no longer asks you to be non-resident everywhere else.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Tax residence is the question of which country taxes you as a resident. It is not the question of which card is in your wallet. The Tax Department's page on residency and domicile is the public text this guide follows for the tests. PwC's Tax Facts and Figures 2026 is used only where it summarises a 2026 change or a rate, and it is named as a summary.

Two tests, not a feeling of having moved

An individual is a Cyprus tax resident, the Department says, under the 183-day rule or the 60-day rule in the Income Tax Law of 2002.

The 183-day rule is the plain one. Stay in Cyprus for one or more periods that exceed 183 days in total in the tax year, and the Department treats you as resident. The tax year is the calendar year.

The 60-day rule is for someone who does not reach 183 days. On the Department's page it requires all of the following. You remain in Cyprus at least 60 days in the tax year. You do not reside for more than 183 days in another country. You carry on a business in Cyprus, or you are employed in Cyprus, or you hold an office there. You maintain a permanent home in Cyprus that you own or rent. If that business or employment stops during the year, the Department says you are not a Cyprus tax resident for that year under this rule. Days alone do not pass the test.

Days are counted the same way for both rules, on the Department's page. The day you arrive is a day in Cyprus. The day you leave is a day outside. Arriving and leaving on the same day is a day in Cyprus. Leaving and coming back on the same day is a day outside. A diary matters more than a guess in December.

What 1 January 2026 took out

Until the end of 2025, commentaries described a further condition: a person using the 60-day rule had to not be tax resident in any other state for that year. PwC's 2026 booklet and a Legal 500 note of 3 November 2026 say Law 244(I)/2025 removed that condition from 1 January 2026. The law was published in the Official Gazette on 31 December 2025. The other conditions stay. The Department's own list, as published on its page, matches the shorter test: it states the 60 days, the 183-day cap in another country, the Cyprus tie of business, job or office, and the home. It does not, on that page, add a ban on being tax resident elsewhere.

A double-tax treaty can still decide a competition between two residence claims. This guide does not apply a treaty to a named pair of countries.

Being resident brings worldwide income into the Cyprus income-tax frame, subject to exemptions, treaties and relief. It does not by itself produce a tax number you can quote. The bands PwC states for 2026 are nil on the first €22,000 of chargeable income, 20 percent from €22,001 to €32,000, 25 percent to €42,000, 30 percent to €72,000 and 35 percent over €72,000. Up to 31 December 2025 the nil band, in the same booklet, was the first €19,500. Those are PwC's figures for the amended law. The statute in Greek is the authority if they ever diverge.

What non-dom status then does to defence contribution on dividends and interest is a second subject, in Cyprus non-dom tax in 2026. Salary exemptions for a first job are described there as well, as conditions, not as a result.

What this page will not collapse

A yellow slip, a pink slip or a nomad permit is an immigration document. The clocks for those are in EU residence in Cyprus and, for the nomad scheme, in the digital nomad guide. The site's relocation guide says a residence card does not create tax residence, and it is the place that points to the filing rule for a resident aged 25 to 70 with no income. This page does not restate that filing rule as a statute it has quoted. Follow the source that guide names.

Cost is a third column. Contributions and bands are not a rent. The cost of living guide keeps them apart on purpose.

Nothing here is a computation of your tax. A day count you have not written down is not a day count the Department will accept.

Questions readers ask

Does a yellow slip or a pink slip make me tax resident?

No. Immigration status and tax residence are different tests. The Tax Department's page is the tax test. The relocation guide on this site separates the two clocks.

How are days counted?

The Tax Department says the day of arrival counts as a day in Cyprus and the day of departure counts as a day outside. Arrival and departure on the same day count as a day in Cyprus. Departure and return on the same day count as a day outside.

What are the four conditions of the 60-day rule?

At least 60 days in Cyprus, no more than 183 days in any other single country, a business, job or office in Cyprus, and a permanent home you own or rent. If the business or the job ends during the year, the Department says you are not tax resident for that year under this rule.

What are the 2026 income-tax bands?

PwC's Tax Facts and Figures 2026, summarising the amended law, states nil on the first €22,000, then 20, 25, 30 and 35 percent, with 35 percent above €72,000. PwC is not the statute. Check the assessment.

Do I have to file if I have no income?

The relocation guide on this site says that from the 2026 tax year a resident aged 25 to 70 files even with no income, and it points to MyCyTax for that filing rule. This page does not repeat the rule as if it had re-read the statute. Follow that guide's source.
