Cost of living in Cyprus in 2026: what is published, and what is not

No official source used here prints one monthly budget for Limassol or Paphos. Cystat and Eurostat publish price indexes. Rent, power and contributions are separate bills.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 25 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

Search interest in "cost of living in Cyprus" is real. A single monthly figure that would answer it, for Limassol or for Paphos or for a family of a stated size, is not in the official sources this guide uses. The relocation guide on this site already refuses to print one, for that reason. This page says what is published instead, and what a reader still has to price for themselves.

Indexes, not a shopping basket with your name on it

The Statistical Service of Cyprus, Cystat, publishes the consumer-price statistics for the Republic. Eurostat publishes the harmonised index of consumer prices so that EU countries can be set next to each other. Both are indexes. An index says how a defined basket moved. It does not say what your landlord will ask, what a school will charge or what a clinic outside GeSY will bill.

When Cystat releases a month, the useful habit is to read that release, not a screenshot of an older one. CyNews 24 reported the October 2026 figures in inflation in October 2026. That story is a news report of one month. It is not a forecast for 2027, and it is not a budget.

The bills that are rules, and the bills that are quotes

Some costs are written down as rates. They are still not a cost of living, because they apply only if you are the person the rule describes.

Income tax for a Cyprus tax resident in 2026, as PwC's Tax Facts and Figures 2026 summarises the amended law, starts with a nil band on the first €22,000 of chargeable income, then 20 percent to €32,000, 25 percent to €42,000, 30 percent to €72,000 and 35 percent above that. PwC is a published summary, not the statute. The Tax Department's page is the residence test that decides whether those bands apply at all. Both are linked from Cyprus tax residency in 2026. A person who is not tax resident is not billed as if they were.

GeSY contributions are a second published scale, on the Health Insurance Organisation's financing page: 2.65 percent for an employee, 2.90 percent for an employer, 4 percent for a self-employed person, with a cap of €180,000 of annual income. They are not a premium you shop. Who pays, and who is actually a beneficiary, is GeSY for foreigners. A private policy for the months before any public enrolment is a third bill. Its price is on the insurer's site and is not printed here. The difference between the products is in international health insurance.

Social insurance is another rate with a ceiling. PwC's 2026 booklet states 8.8 percent for the employee and 8.8 percent for the employer, on earnings up to €68,904 in 2026. That is a contribution, not a salary. What jobs do and do not publish as pay is in jobs and pay in Cyprus.

Electricity is a tariff, not an index. The Electricity Authority of Cyprus publishes its charges. This page does not copy a kilowatt-hour price, because the bill depends on the tariff and the use. Fuel prices move often enough that a guide should point at the receipt, not freeze a number. How a receipt is broken down was reported in the fuel-receipt story.

Rent is a contract

There is no official average rent for a two-bedroom flat in Limassol on the sources above. Listings are asks, not a statistic. A written tenancy, what a deposit is and is not, and the short-term rental rules are in renting in Cyprus. Buying is a different stack of taxes and permissions, in buying property as a foreigner.

How to keep the numbers honest

Put rent, power and water, groceries, transport, contributions and any private premium in separate lines. Do not add a line that does not apply to your status. A visitor's month and a tax resident's month are not the same column. Towns are not the same column either: the coast and Nicosia do not share weather or, in practice, rents, which is why the weather guide and this page both refuse a single island figure.

A plug adapter is a shopping item, not part of the index. Cyprus uses the UK square-pin socket. Prices for an adapter are whatever the shop shows that day.

This page will not be updated with a made-up basket. When Cystat or Eurostat publishes a new index, the honest change is a link to that release, plus a new updated date.

Questions readers ask

What does a month in Cyprus cost?

This page does not give a number. The Statistical Service publishes consumer-price indexes, not a ready budget for an expat household in one town. A forum average is not that index.

Where do I see whether prices are rising?

Cystat publishes the national figures. Eurostat publishes the harmonised index so countries can be compared. CyNews 24's report on the October 2026 inflation release is linked in the text. The next release replaces it.

Is rent included in the index?

Housing costs enter consumer-price indexes under the statistician's rules, which are not the same as the rent in one contract. The rent you will pay is the figure in that contract. There is no official island-wide rent on this page.

Which regular bills are set in public rules?

GeSY contribution rates and the income-tax bands are published rules, not shop prices. They are summarised in the GeSY and tax guides, with the sources named there. Electricity tariffs are on the Electricity Authority's site and change.

Can I add rent, tax and a health premium into one cost of living?

You can keep them in separate columns. Adding them into one number hides which of them applies to you. A visitor does not pay the same contributions as a tax resident with a salary.
