Tourist arrivals are down 5.6 per cent over ten months, and October lost 29,000

The statistical service counted 3.91 million arrivals from January to October, almost 233,000 fewer than a year earlier. October wiped out September's gain.

CyNews 24 Editorial TeamPublished: 17 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)Updated: 17 November 2026, 09:00 (EET)

The statistical service published Tuesday's arrival figures, and Phileleftheros and the Cyprus Mail agree on the headline count. Cyprus recorded 508,301 tourist arrivals in October, against 537,744 in October 2025: down 5.5 per cent, or 29,443 people. From January to October the total was 3,909,839, against 4,142,534 a year earlier. That is a 5.6 per cent fall and 232,695 fewer visits — "almost 233,000", as Phileleftheros puts it.

The October drop is larger than September's rise. The Cyprus Mail notes that September was a record for that month and added 10,254 arrivals. September and October together were still 19,189 visits short of the same two months a year earlier. The ten-month total is nevertheless 4.9 per cent above January–October 2024, when arrivals were 3,727,196. October 2026 is also above October 2024, when the island recorded 459,106: this October is 10.7 per cent higher.

Britain remains the largest market, 32.4 per cent of October arrivals. British arrivals were 164,512, down 2.5 per cent from 168,792 a year earlier and 1.2 per cent below the 166,588 of two years ago. The biggest fall in numbers was Israel. After a 31.4 per cent rise in September, October was down 17.5 per cent: 49,091 against 59,508. That is still more than double the 24,088 recorded in October 2024.

France had the steepest percentage fall among the markets the service lists: down 55.8 per cent, to 4,783 from 10,824, and 44.6 per cent below October 2024. The Cyprus Mail says it was the third month running in which French arrivals fell by at least 46 per cent on the year. Israel and France together accounted for 16,458 fewer visits, 56 per cent of the net October decline.

Poland nearly caught Germany. German arrivals were 40,295, down 3.6 per cent on the year but 15.7 per cent above October 2024. Polish arrivals were 40,070, up 10.5 per cent and 8.3 per cent above 2024. The gap between the two markets was 225 people, and each had 7.9 per cent of October. Poland added more arrivals than any other market on the service's list. Greece rose 9.9 per cent, to 22,014. Larnaca and Paphos airports handled 1.44 million arriving and departing passengers in October, down 3.1 per cent; over ten months they handled 11.8 million, down 2.9 per cent on 2025 and 8 per cent above 2024.

Holidays were still the main reason for a visit: 80.4 per cent of October arrivals, against 81.8 per cent a year earlier. The share visiting friends and relatives rose to 12 per cent from 11, and business travel to 7.4 per cent from 7.1. Residents of Cyprus made fewer trips abroad: 154,263 returns, against 158,026, down 2.4 per cent, but 12.5 per cent above October 2024. Greece was still the first destination, with 48,827 returns and 31.7 per cent.
